The VLA framework
Figure 2 separates three roles
图 2 把系统分成三个角色。
The high level policy proposes a language subtask
高层策略提出语言子任务。
The world model generates images of a near future goal
世界模型生成近期目标图像。
The VLA sees current images, joint states, and compressed visual history
VLA 输入当前图像、关节状态和压缩历史。
Its flow matching action expert generates motor commands
流匹配动作专家生成控制指令。
Goal images describe the destination. The action expert chooses how to reach it
目标图像描述终点，动作专家决定怎样到达。

Prompt composition
Figure 3 shows the expanded prompt
图 3 展示扩展后的提示结构。
It combines the task, a subtask, visual subgoals, and episode metadata
包含任务、子任务、视觉子目标和轨迹元数据。
A control label selects joint space or end effector commands
控制标签选择关节空间或末端控制。
Prompt dropout lets the model combine optional prompt components at runtime
提示随机丢弃让模型能按需组合可选输入。

Why metadata matters
Metadata describes speed, quality, and mistakes in each training episode
元数据描述每条轨迹的速度、质量和失误。
That distinguishes good behavior from slower or unsuccessful attempts
它把良好行为与低效、失败尝试区分开。
At runtime, the prompt asks for high quality and no mistakes
执行时，提示指定高质量、无失误。
Those labels steer behavior. They do not guarantee a successful outcome
标签引导行为，但不保证任务成功。

Coaching a new task
For a new air fryer task, separate three stages
新空气炸锅任务，要分清三个阶段。
A single zero shot instruction produces only partial execution in the website example
官网示例中，一句零样本指令只完成了部分步骤。
Step by step language coaching helps the robot complete the sequence
逐步语言指导帮助机器人完成任务序列。
Figure 15 tests this coached setting
图 15 测试的是有人指导的设置。

From coaching to autonomy
Figure 16 is the next stage
图 16 是下一阶段。
Coaching episodes train a high level policy to generate the subtasks automatically
指导轨迹用于训练自动生成子任务的高层策略。
That creates an autonomous task policy without new teleoperated action demonstrations
无需新增遥操动作示范，即可形成自主任务策略。
But it uses coaching data and extra high level training
但它使用了指导数据和额外的高层训练。
Keep that distinction when interpreting zero shot claims
解读零样本能力时，要保留这个区别。

The controlled ablation
Figure 18 tests the data argument
图 18 是检验数据论点的重要实验。
Adding lower quality data can hurt a model without metadata
没有元数据时，加入低质量数据可能降低表现。
With metadata, performance improves as the dataset expands
有元数据时，表现随数据规模扩大而改善。
Removing the most diverse 20% hurts more than removing a random 20%
移除最多样的 20%，比随机移除 20% 更伤性能。
The amounts match, so this comparison tests diversity beyond volume
移除数量相同，因此能考察数据量之外的多样性。

What the evidence establishes
The paper reports 60–80% success for unseen tasks or robot-task combinations
论文报告：未见任务或任务本体组合成功率约 60–80%。
Related skills may still exist elsewhere in the large training mixture
相关技能仍可能存在于庞大训练数据的其他部分。
My reading: richer context makes diverse experience more usable
我的理解：更丰富的上下文，让多样经验更可用。
That is the connection from RECAP to a steerable generalist
这就是从 RECAP 到可引导通用模型的联系。
