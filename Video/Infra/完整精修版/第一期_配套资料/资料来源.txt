# DoF Infra EP.01 — World Models / narrative upgrade

Core question: before moving a cup past an obstacle, how can a prediction change the robot's decision?
The persistent cup/robot/obstacle diagram is an original explanatory illustration. Candidate paths, reward scores and uncertainty rings are illustrative, not measured experiments. It is not footage of a reproduced system.

Primary sources (already retrieved in the project; source figures inspected for this revision):

1. DayDreamer: World Models for Physical Robot Learning. Wu et al., CoRL 2022 / PMLR 205 (2023).
Paper: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/wu23c.html
PDF: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/wu23c/wu23c.pdf
Project: https://danijar.com/project/daydreamer/
Figure 3: real sequences train the latent model; imagined rollouts train actor/critic. Distinguish imagination during training from online action-sequence planning.
Official clips: quadruped early learning and authors' robot-platform overview. These are physical robots; behaviors are learned separately through interaction. No claim of a general pretrained controller or fully unattended deployment.

2. TD-MPC2: Scalable, Robust World Models for Continuous Control. Hansen et al., ICLR 2024.
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.16828
Project: https://www.tdmpc2.com/
Figure 3: control-centric latent dynamics, predicted rewards and terminal values; online action-sequence optimization, execute first action and replan. Reconstruction of future frames is not required.
Official clips: Stack Cube and Humanoid Run are SIMULATION benchmarks, clearly labelled on screen.
https://www.tdmpc2.com/videos/tdmpc2-stack-cube.mp4
https://www.tdmpc2.com/videos/tdmpc2-humanoid-run.mp4

3. V-JEPA 2: Self-Supervised Video Models Enable Understanding, Prediction and Planning. Assran et al., 2025.
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09985
Code: https://github.com/facebookresearch/vjepa2
Official demonstration: https://ai.meta.com/blog/v-jepa-2-world-model-benchmarks/
Figures 2 and 7: video pretraining then robot action post-training; action-conditioned endpoint prediction compared against goal-image features. The physical clip is the actual execution excerpt at source 24–29s, not the preceding explanatory animation. Source paper reports ~16s/action planning on RTX 4090; edited video speed is not presented as planner speed. Supplied goal images are explicitly stated.

4. RoboDreamer: Learning Compositional World Models for Robot Imagination. Zhou et al., ICML 2024.
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12377
Project: https://umass-embodied-agi.github.io/robodreamer/
Figure 3: language composition and visual conditions generate a desired video plan. Paired stack-blocks clips retain GENERATED PLAN and RLBench SIMULATION labels. They are not evidence of successful physical deployment.
