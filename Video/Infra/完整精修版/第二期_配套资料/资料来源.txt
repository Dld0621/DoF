Interpretation boundaries
The blue tabletop cup, gripper, feature patches, token boxes, moving curves and arrows are DoF explanatory illustrations. They are not calibrated robot geometry, attention maps, logged control values or recovered internal model reasoning. The moving flow curves are not recorded joint trajectories. Real robot demos illustrate behavior only, not aggregate success or transfer to arbitrary unseen robot bodies. Deployment feedback does not imply online weight updates.
