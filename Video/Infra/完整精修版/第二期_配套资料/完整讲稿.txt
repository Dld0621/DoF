01. Put the blue cup on the plate. Easy to say. But what should the robot do first?
把蓝色杯子放到盘子上。说起来简单，机器人第一步该怎么动？

02. Watch this OpenVLA example. The first grasp tips the cup. The robot tries again.
看这段 OpenVLA 官方演示：第一次抓取碰倒杯子，机器人随后重试。

03. That gap, between a sentence and a useful movement, is our subject today. DoF Infra, episode two.
从一句话到有效动作，中间这段距离，就是 DoF 认知 Infra 第二期的主题。

04. Keep that same cup and plate in mind. A camera gives us pixels. The instruction tells us which relationship to create.
始终记住这只杯子和盘子。相机提供像素，指令指定我们希望建立的关系。

05. Change the instruction to move the plate, and the image stays the same. The desired action changes.
把指令改成移动盘子，图像没变，但需要执行的动作变了。

06. A vision-language-action model learns this conditional mapping: given what I see and what I am asked, which action fits?
视觉语言动作模型学习的就是这种条件映射：结合所见和任务要求，选择合适的动作。

07. Here is the original OpenVLA architecture. Follow the image upward: DINOv2 and SigLIP extract complementary visual features.
这是 OpenVLA 原论文架构。沿图像输入向上看，DINOv2 与 SigLIP 提取互补的视觉特征。

08. A projector maps these features into the language model. Image features and instruction tokens now condition the same prediction.
投影层把这些特征映射到语言模型中，图像特征与指令 token 共同约束预测。

09. The important change is at the output. We need a robot command. We do not need an explanation of what a cup is.
关键变化发生在输出端：我们需要机器人命令，而不是一段关于杯子的解释。

10. So what is a command? In the original OpenVLA setup: three translation values, three rotation values, and one gripper value.
命令具体是什么？原始 OpenVLA 使用三个平移量、三个旋转量，以及一个夹爪量。

11. Those numbers only make sense with the right coordinate frame, scaling, and control interval. The controller turns them into motion.
这些数字必须对应正确的坐标系、缩放和控制间隔，控制器才能把它们转成运动。

12. OpenVLA divides each action dimension into 256 bins. A continuous value becomes a discrete token, predicted like a next word.
OpenVLA 把每个动作维度划分为 256 个区间，将连续数值变成离散 token，像预测下一个词一样生成它。

13. Decode the seven tokens and you recover one control action. That is a representation choice, not a language sentence sent to the motors.
解码七个 token，就得到一次控制动作。这是一种动作表示，并非把自然语言句子发给电机。

14. Now compare pi zero. The original figure still has a vision-language backbone, but adds a continuous action expert and robot state.
再看 π0。原图仍然包含视觉语言骨干，但加入了连续动作专家和机器人状态。

15. Instead of emitting only the next action, pi zero predicts a chunk of fifty future control steps in its reported setup.
在论文设置中，π0 预测的是包含 50 个未来控制步的动作块，而不只是下一步。

16. How? Start with noise. A learned flow repeatedly updates the entire chunk, conditioned on images, language, and the robot state.
如何生成？从噪声开始，学习到的流以图像、语言和机器人状态为条件，反复更新整个动作块。

17. These moving curves explain the idea. They are not logged joint trajectories. The paper figure shows where the action expert sits.
这些动态曲线用于解释机制，不是真实记录的关节轨迹。原论文图标出了动作专家所在的位置。

18. Do not confuse two separate choices: discrete versus continuous values, and one action versus an action chunk. Tokens can encode chunks too.
这里有两个独立维度：离散值还是连续值，以及单步动作还是动作块。token 同样可以编码动作块。

19. FAST is a useful counterexample: it compresses continuous action sequences into discrete tokens. The categories can cross.
FAST 就是一个反例：它把连续动作序列压缩为离散 token。两种划分可以交叉组合。

20. Back to our cup. After execution, take another observation. The cup may have moved. The next prediction must use the new situation.
回到杯子。执行后重新观测：杯子可能已经移动，下一次预测必须依据新的场景。

21. In this official reattempt, fresh visual input allows another try. The video shows behavior, not the model's hidden reasoning.
在这段官方重试演示中，新的视觉输入支持再次尝试。视频展示的是行为，不是模型的内部推理过程。

22. And new input does not mean new weights. Correcting a movement during inference is different from learning from that failure in training.
新输入不等于新权重。推理过程中修正动作，与训练时从失败经验中学习，是两件不同的事。

23. Training uses demonstrations to change model parameters. Deployment repeatedly queries those parameters with the latest observation.
训练使用示范数据更新模型参数；部署则用最新观测，反复调用已经学到的参数。

24. Now look beyond the nice demo. OpenVLA reports direct evaluation on its training robot platforms, and fine-tuning on Franka tasks.
再看演示之外的条件：OpenVLA 在训练所用机器人平台上直接评测，也在 Franka 任务上进行微调。

25. Ask what changed: the object, the instruction, the scene, or the robot body. A successful clip cannot answer every generalization question.
应当追问：改变的是物体、指令、场景，还是机器人本体？单个成功片段无法回答所有泛化问题。

26. When you meet another VLA, trace three things: what goes in, how actions are represented, and how execution returns new evidence.
以后读到新的 VLA，沿三条线追踪：输入是什么，动作怎样表示，执行如何带回新的证据。

27. That gives you a framework for reading the next paper. The source papers and our learning map are linked in the DoF project.
有了这条线，再读下一篇论文就有了框架。原论文与学习路线收录在 DoF 项目中。
