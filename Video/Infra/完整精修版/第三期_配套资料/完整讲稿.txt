一直看着这只杯子。问题改变，需要的表示也可能改变。

What did the camera actually measure?
The camera sees an image. With depth, each valid pixel also records the visible surface depth.
相机得到图像。加入深度后，每个有效像素也记录了可见表面的深度。
Calibration turns that pixel and depth into an X, Y, Z position. Repeat this for many pixels: a point cloud.
标定把像素与深度变成 X、Y、Z 坐标。对许多像素重复这个过程，就得到点云。
Now walk around the cup. Those missing points on the back were never measured. Moving the display camera cannot reveal new evidence.
现在绕到杯子后面。背面缺失的点从未被测量过，旋转展示视角不会产生新证据。

What did the camera actually measure?
Points can carry color, normals, or confidence: local orientation and how much to trust each sample.
点可以携带颜色、法向或置信度：局部朝向，以及每个采样有多可信。
Depth maps keep samples arranged by camera pixels. Height fields store one height per ground location, so overhangs need another representation.
深度图按相机像素组织采样。高度场每个地面位置只存一个高度，因此悬挑结构需要其他表示。

What connects the samples?
Give each point an oriented patch and you have surfels: little pieces of surface, without a shared triangle network.
给每个点加上有朝向的小面片，就得到 Surfel：局部表面片，不必共享三角连接。
A mesh makes connections explicit. Vertices join through edges and faces. Watch what happens if we connect the wrong neighbors.
Mesh 显式给出连接关系：顶点通过边和面连接。看看连错邻居会怎样。
This triangle seals the handle. The surface looks complete, yet the opening has disappeared. Reconstruction introduced an assumption, not a measurement.
这个三角面封住了杯柄。表面看似完整，开口却消失了。重建引入的是假设，不是测量。

What connects the samples?
Remove that bridge. Now the same points support a different surface. More detail does not automatically mean more trustworthy geometry.
去掉错误连接，同一批点可以支持不同的表面。细节更多，不意味着几何一定更可信。
ElasticFusion builds a dense surfel map from RGB and depth. Repeated observations update its surface estimate.
ElasticFusion 从 RGB 与深度构建稠密 Surfel 地图，连续观测更新表面估计。

Can the fingers pass through?
The gripper needs a spatial query. Occupancy asks whether a location is occupied. Crucially, observed free space and unknown space are different.
夹爪需要查询空间。占据表示回答某个位置是否被占用。已观测空闲与未知空间，必须区分。
A depth ray supports free space before its first return. Behind it, the map may know nothing.
深度射线支持首个回波前的空闲空间判断。后方可能依然未知。
Voxels divide space into cells. They can store occupancy, distance, or features. An octree subdivides selectively, saving resolution for useful detail.
体素把空间切成单元，可存占据、距离或特征。八叉树选择性细分，把分辨率留给需要的细节。

Can the fingers pass through?
But free or occupied is not enough. How close is the finger to the wall? A distance field answers that.
但空闲或占用还不够。手指离杯壁多近？距离场回答这个问题。
An SDF adds an inside or outside sign. An unsigned distance field keeps distance without requiring a global interior.
SDF 额外区分内部与外部；无符号距离场只保留距离，不要求定义全局内部。
A truncated SDF fuses depth near surfaces. For planning, an E SDF estimates Euclidean clearance to mapped obstacles.
TSDF 在表面附近融合深度；规划使用的 ESDF 则估计到已建图障碍物的欧氏间距。

What if the task is design or simulation?
Suppose we want to redesign the handle. CAD keeps dimensions and constraints editable; NURBS describe smooth parametric curves and surfaces.
如果要重新设计杯柄，CAD 保留可编辑的尺寸和约束；NURBS 描述光滑的参数曲线与曲面。
Constructive solid geometry combines simple solids by union, intersection, or subtraction. Subtracting a shape can create the opening deliberately.
构造实体几何通过并集、交集或差集组合简单实体。减去一个形状，可以有意做出开口。
For deformation, a surface alone misses the material inside. Volume meshes divide that material into cells; physics supplies how they respond.
要模拟形变，仅有表面还缺少内部材料。体网格把材料切成单元，物理模型决定它们如何响应。

What if we only need a new view?
Now change the question. We want a photograph from a new camera position. Appearance becomes the target, rather than finger clearance.
现在换个问题：从新的相机位置生成照片。目标变成外观，而不是手指间距。
NeRF queries density and view dependent color along rays, then integrates them into pixels. The original figure shows that rendering process.
NeRF 沿射线查询密度与视角相关颜色，再积分得到像素。原论文这张图展示了渲染过程。
3D Gaussian Splatting instead optimizes spatial Gaussian primitives, projects them, and blends their contributions into an image.
3D Gaussian Splatting 优化空间中的高斯基元，再投影并混合它们对图像的贡献。

What if we only need a new view?
2D Gaussian Splatting uses oriented disks for stronger surface consistency. Light fields describe rays. These choices organize appearance differently.
2D Gaussian Splatting 用有朝向的圆盘增强表面一致性；光场描述光线。它们以不同方式组织外观。
A convincing rendered view does not certify collision safety. The model must answer your actual question.
渲染得逼真，不能证明碰撞安全。模型必须回答实际的问题。

Who moves, and what changes?
Once the hand moves, structure matters. Skeletons organize joints; parametric models such as MANO connect pose and shape to a hand mesh.
手动起来后，结构也很重要。骨架组织关节；MANO 等参数模型把姿态、形状连接到手部网格。
A scene graph can say the cup is on the table, and the hand is approaching it. Relations complement metric geometry.
场景图可以表达杯子在桌上，手正靠近杯子。关系信息补充了度量几何。
Add time, and geometry or appearance can change. Scene flow, tracked objects, and dynamic fields encode different kinds of motion.
加入时间，几何或外观都可能变化。场景流、目标跟踪与动态场表达不同层面的运动。

Choose by the question.
So this is a toolbox, not a ladder. Observe with samples, connect a surface, query space, render appearance, and track structure.
所以它是工具箱，不是一条升级阶梯：用采样观测，连接表面，查询空间，渲染外观，跟踪结构。
Explicit geometry stores elements. Implicit fields answer queries. Either can be learned; neural versus analytic is a separate choice.
显式几何存储元素，隐式场回答查询。两者都可以学习；神经表示与解析表示是另一条划分维度。

Choose by the question.
For our cup, preserve what was measured, mark what is unknown, and test the gripper against the geometry that matters.
回到这只杯子：保留测量证据，标清未知区域，并用与任务相关的几何检验夹爪。
