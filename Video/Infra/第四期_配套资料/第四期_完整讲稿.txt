DoF Infra EP.04 | Camera-Only Hand Tracking: From Pixels to Contact

01 Your hand moves. Will the robot follow?
Your fingers close around a cup. The robot almost does.
你的手捏住杯子，机器人却差了一点。

For camera-only teleoperation, which three-dimensional tracking method is actually more accurate?
只用摄像头，哪种三维手部动捕更准？

Welcome to DoF Infra, episode four.
欢迎来到 DoF Infra 第四期。

We will follow one pinch, from pixels to physical contact.
这次跟着一次捏合，从像素走到真实接触。

02 Start with more useful views.
For precision, I would start with synchronized, calibrated cameras from different angles.
精度优先，我会先选同步、标定的多视角相机。

Use depth where it is reliable, then fit a consistent hand model.
深度可靠时加入深度，再拟合连续的手模型。

That is an engineering starting point, not a universal winner.
这是工程起点，不是通用精度冠军。

The object, viewing angles, and control delay can change the answer.
物体、观察角度和控制延迟都会改变结果。

03 From depth pixels to visible points.
Start with a depth frame. Isolate the hand from the object and background.
从一帧深度图开始，先分离人手、物体与背景。

Each valid depth pixel, together with camera intrinsics, maps to a three-dimensional surface point.
每个有效深度像素，结合相机内参，就能还原为三维表面点。

Together, these points sample the visible surface, not the hidden side.
这些点采样可见表面，并不包含被挡住的背面。

Calibrated views can contribute more observed surfaces in one coordinate frame.
标定后的视角可以在同一坐标系补充可见表面。

04 Surface points are not finger joints.
A point cloud records surfaces. Joint centers usually sit inside the hand.
点云记录表面；关节中心通常位于手内部。

A model estimates a skeleton from image or depth evidence and anatomical constraints.
模型结合图像或深度证据与人体结构约束，估计骨架。

Separate global wrist motion from local finger articulation. The same pinch can move anywhere in space.
把手腕整体运动与手指局部弯曲分开；同一捏合可以发生在不同位置。

05 A mesh is a fitted, articulated surface.
An articulated mesh represents the hand with vertices and connected faces.
关节驱动的 Mesh 用顶点和相连的面来表示人手。

In MANO, shape describes hand proportions; pose describes joint rotations. Skinning moves the surface with the joints.
在 MANO 中，形状描述手的比例，姿态描述关节旋转；蒙皮让表面随关节变形。

Fit the model to observed landmarks or surfaces, with anatomical priors.
用观测到的关键点或表面，加上人体结构先验来拟合模型。

A plausible hidden finger is still inferred, not measured.
看似合理的隐藏手指，仍然是推断而非测量。

06 Motion is a sequence of consistent hand states.
Reconstructing one frame gives a pose. Motion capture tracks how that pose changes over time.
重建一帧得到姿态；动捕跟踪姿态随时间的变化。

Keep finger identities consistent, and track wrist motion and articulation together.
保持手指身份一致，同时跟踪手腕运动和关节变化。

A fixed template gives vertices consistent identities; their positions still have to be estimated in every frame.
固定模板让顶点编号一致，但每一帧的位置仍需重新估计。

Temporal constraints reduce jitter, but predicted motion during occlusion still carries uncertainty.
时序约束能抑制抖动，但遮挡期间预测的运动仍有不确定性。

07 These are representations, not mandatory steps.
There is no compulsory point-cloud-to-mesh pipeline.
点云到网格，并不是所有方法都必须走的流程。

HaMeR predicts a parametric hand mesh directly from an RGB image.
HaMeR 直接从 RGB 图像预测参数化手模型。

DexPilot combines depth observations, estimated joints, and articulated model tracking.
DexPilot 结合深度观测、关节估计与关节模型跟踪。

For teleoperation, extract wrist and fingertip targets, then retarget them to the robot.
遥操作还要提取手腕与指尖目标，再映射到机器人。

08 One image leaves depth uncertain.
A single RGB camera is a convenient first prototype.
单目 RGB 很适合快速搭建原型。

But different three-dimensional hands can project to similar images.
不同的三维手势，可能投影成相似图像。

HaMeR reports six millimeters on FreiHAND after Procrustes alignment.
HaMeR 的对齐后关节误差为六毫米。

That alignment removes translation, rotation, and scale differences.
这种对齐会消除平移、旋转和尺度差异。

It does not promise six-millimeter accuracy in your robot coordinates.
它不保证机器人坐标下也有六毫米精度。

09 A second view must reveal something new.
A second view helps when it reveals what the first camera cannot see.
第二视角的价值，在于看见第一台的盲区。

Calibrate every camera into the same frame and synchronize exposures.
所有相机要统一坐标，并同步曝光时间。

Otherwise, a moving fingertip is fused from different places or times.
否则，运动指尖会被跨位置、跨时间地融合。

HO-Cap illustrates calibrated multi-view reconstruction. Its annotation pipeline is not live control.
HO-Cap 展示多视角重建；标注流程不等于实时控制。

10 Compare within the same experiment.
AnyTeleop offers a useful comparison inside one system.
AnyTeleop 给出了同一系统内的对比。

In its simulated piano task, wrong-key rates fell from twenty-eight point one percent,
仿真钢琴任务中，误按率从百分之二十八点一，

to twenty-one point eight, then twelve point five,
降至二十一点八，再到十二点五，

using RGB, RGB-D, and two RGB-D cameras.
分别对应 RGB、RGB-D 和双 RGB-D。

One trained operator, ten trials per setup. Task evidence, not sensor error in millimeters.
同一操作者每组十次；误按率不等于毫米精度。

11 Unknown fingers should stay uncertain.
Now turn the hand sideways and hold the cup.
现在把手转向侧面，再握住杯子。

A model can predict hidden joints from anatomy and recent motion.
模型能用手部结构与近期运动推断隐藏关节。

Prediction is useful, but it is not a new observation.
预测很有用，但并不等于新的观测。

When confidence drops, reduce motion, hold, or stop according to the task.
置信度下降时，应按任务减速、保持或停止。

Recovery should be gradual, rather than snapping the robot to a new pose.
恢复跟踪要渐进，避免机器人突然跳姿态。

12 Preserve the grasp, not every angle.
Tracking is only the first half. Human and robot hands have different geometry.
动捕只完成一半，人手与机器人的结构不同。

Copying joint angles can leave the robot pinch open.
直接复制关节角，机器人可能捏不拢。

Retarget fingertip relationships, thumb opposition, and wrist pose, within robot limits.
应在关节限制内映射指尖关系、拇指对掌与腕姿。

Then verify actual robot motion and contact. A virtual touch is not a measured grip force.
再验证实际运动与接触；虚拟触碰不是实测握力。

13 Fast frames can still produce slow control.
Measure delay across the whole chain: exposure, estimation, communication, and robot response.
延迟要贯穿曝光、估计、通信与机器人响应。

A high frame rate alone does not tell you that delay.
帧率高，不代表整条链路延迟低。

Smoothing can reduce jitter while making fast pinches arrive late.
平滑能抑制抖动，也可能让快速捏合滞后。

Report both typical delay and the slowest tail, such as the ninety-fifth percentile.
既要报典型延迟，也要看第九十五百分位。

14 Test the failure that matters.
Before choosing cameras, repeat the same grasp under the same controller.
选相机前，用同一控制器重复相同抓取。

Test open fingers, precise pinches, side views, occlusion, and reappearance.
测试张手、精细捏合、侧视、遮挡与重新出现。

Check absolute wrist and fingertip error against an independent reference where visible.
参考可见时，独立测量腕部与指尖的绝对误差。

Keep missing reference frames explicit. Count tracking loss, delay, failed grasps, and drops.
明确参考缺失帧，并统计掉踪、延迟、失败与掉物。

15 Choose the system that survives the grasp.
My camera-only recommendation: RGB for a quick prototype, RGB-D for a fixed workspace,
纯视觉建议：RGB 快速验证，RGB-D 定点起步，

and calibrated multi-view capture when precision and occlusion justify the setup.
精度与遮挡要求更高时，升级标定的多视角。

Start with two complementary views; add more only when measured failures justify them.
先用两个互补视角，再按实测失败补充相机。

The goal is a repeatable grasp, not just a convincing digital hand.
目标是可重复的抓取，而不只是好看的数字手。
