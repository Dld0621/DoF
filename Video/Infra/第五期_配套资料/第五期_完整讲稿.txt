DoF Infra EP05｜动捕的真值从哪来
Ground Truth: How Motion Capture Builds Its Reference

英文旁白 / 中英双语讲稿

01 / 00:01  How do we know a hand tracker is accurate?

Two hand trackers report tiny errors. Will they place a robot fingertip equally well?
两个动捕系统误差都很小，机器人指尖就一样准吗？

First, ask what each one measures, and where its reference comes from.
先问清楚：测的是什么，参考答案又从哪来？

Welcome to DoF Infra, episode five: how motion capture gets its ground truth.
欢迎来到 DoF Infra 第五期：动捕的真值从哪来。

We will build the measurement chain, then unpack the major tracking routes.
这次先搭起测量链，再逐条拆解主要动捕路线。

02 / 00:19  A reference is a measurement pipeline.

A camera observes pixels. An inertial sensor observes rotation rate and specific force.
相机观测像素；惯性传感器观测角速度和比力。

Calibration and reconstruction turn those signals into positions, joint angles, or a mesh.
经过标定与重建，信号才变成位置、关节角或网格。

A dataset may call that fitted result ground truth, with its own uncertainty.
数据集可能把拟合结果称为 GT，但它仍有不确定性。

Robot contact adds another chain: coordinate transforms, retargeting, control, and physical interaction.
机器人接触还要经过坐标变换、重定向、控制和物理交互。

03 / 00:42  Optical markers: from bright spots to 3D.

Start with optical motion capture. Several calibrated infrared cameras observe small reflective markers.
先看光学动捕：多台标定后的红外相机观察反光标记。

Matching the same marker across views lets the system triangulate its three-dimensional position.
确认不同视角中的同一个标记，就能三角化它的三维位置。

HOT3D uses nineteen markers per hand, specified as three-millimeter markers.
HOT3D 每只手使用十九个标记，论文标注尺寸为三毫米。

That number describes marker size. It is not a three-millimeter accuracy guarantee.
三毫米说的是标记尺寸，并不是精度保证。

04 / 01:05  One action. One coordinate frame.

Intrinsics describe the camera projection and distortion. Extrinsics place cameras in a shared frame.
内参描述成像与畸变；外参把相机放入同一坐标系。

A calibrated object establishes scale. Headset and robot frames need their own transforms.
标定物确定尺度；头显和机器人坐标也需要各自的变换。

Check known distances across the actual workspace, including its edges.
要在实际工作区复查已知距离，边缘区域也要检查。

A small calibration residual alone does not validate accuracy on independent measurements.
标定残差很小，还不能代替独立测量验证。

05 / 01:28  A timing error becomes a position error.

Now move the fingertip. Two cameras must observe the same physical instant.
现在让指尖移动：两台相机需要观察同一个真实时刻。

At one meter per second, ten milliseconds of mismatch means roughly ten millimeters.
速度一米每秒时，错开十毫秒就约等于十毫米。

This is a simple illustration, not a measurement of any product.
这是简化示例，并非对某个产品的实测。

Hardware triggering controls sampling; timestamps identify samples. Also check drift and dropped frames.
硬件触发控制采样，时间戳标识样本，还要检查漂移与丢帧。

06 / 01:48  Markers are attached to skin. Joints are inferred.

Surface markers do not sit at the rotation centers hidden inside your fingers.
表面标记并不位于手指内部的关节旋转中心。

Scan or calibrate the person, then fit a skeleton and surface to the observations.
先扫描或标定个体，再用观测拟合骨架与表面。

In HOT3D, fitting a personalized hand model produces the reference motion.
HOT3D 通过拟合个体化手模型，生成参考动作。

The mesh follows that model. Each frame is not a new complete surface scan.
网格随模型运动；每一帧并不是重新扫描了完整表面。

07 / 02:08  Who checks the ground truth?

Check marker identity, reprojection residuals, impossible joint motion, and missing observations.
检查标记身份、重投影残差、异常关节运动和缺失观测。

Keep confidence and validity masks. Interpolated motion should remain distinguishable from measured samples.
保留置信度与有效性标记，区分插值动作和测量样本。

An independent scanner, measurement rig, or manually checked subset can reveal systematic mistakes.
独立扫描、测量装置或人工复核子集，可以揭示系统性错误。

If both methods share the same assumptions, agreement may hide the same bias.
若两套方法共享相同假设，结果一致也可能包含同样偏差。

08 / 02:31  Markerless labels: geometry plus annotation.

Markers are optional. InterHand uses synchronized views and human annotations to bootstrap hand detection.
标记点并非必需：InterHand 用同步多视角和人工标注启动流程。

Consistent two-dimensional keypoints are triangulated; unreliable views can be rejected.
把一致的二维关键点三角化，并剔除不可靠视角。

Automatic detections expand the three-dimensional labels. A later hand-model fit can add meshes.
自动检测扩展三维标注；后续再拟合手模型，可以得到网格。

These labels contain annotation and reconstruction error, especially for occluded anatomical landmarks.
这些标签包含标注与重建误差，被遮挡的解剖点尤其如此。

09 / 02:56  Bring the reference rig into the world.

SHOW3D moves multi-view capture outdoors: two headset views and up to eight backpack views.
SHOW3D 把多视角采集带到户外：两个头戴视角，最多八个背负视角。

Its synchronized cameras support markerless hand and object annotation.
同步相机支持无标记的手部与物体标注。

A separate optical marker setup tracks the headset relative to the moving backpack rig.
另一路光学标记跟踪头显相对移动背架的位置。

So the hands are markerless, but the entire capture system is not.
因此手上没有标记，并不代表整套采集系统完全无标记。

The authors check hand labels against a camera dome and independent manual annotations.
作者用相机穹顶和独立人工标注，检查手部标签质量。

That validation matters: an automatically generated label needs evidence before becoming a trusted reference.
这种验证很关键：自动生成的标签，需要证据才能成为可靠参考。

10 / 03:26  RGB-D: observe a surface, fit a hand.

A depth camera adds measured surface distance to color images.
深度相机在彩色图像之外，增加表面距离观测。

Back-project valid pixels, align the cameras, and fit hand and object models.
反投影有效像素，对齐相机，再拟合人手与物体模型。

HO-Cap combines multiple RGB-D cameras with segmentation and temporal optimization.
HO-Cap 结合多台 RGB-D 相机、分割和时序优化。

Occluded surfaces still need inference. Offline annotation can use future frames that live control cannot.
遮挡表面仍需推断；离线标注还能利用实时控制拿不到的未来帧。

11 / 03:48  Monocular reconstruction: infer missing depth.

With one RGB camera, a learned prior resolves much of the missing depth.
只有一个 RGB 相机时，模型先验承担了许多缺失深度的推断。

WiLoR detects and reconstructs hands. HaWoR also addresses hand motion in world space.
WiLoR 检测并重建人手；HaWoR 进一步处理世界空间中的手部运动。

A plausible hand shape does not by itself establish its absolute scale or position.
手形看起来合理，并不能单独确定绝对尺度与位置。

Check camera motion, root placement, and whether the evaluation removes scale or translation.
需要检查相机运动、手根位置，以及评测是否消除了尺度或平移。

12 / 04:09  Inertial capture: motion without line of sight.

Inertial sensors work through visual occlusion, but sparse sensors leave many joints unobserved.
惯性传感器不怕视觉遮挡，但稀疏布置会留下大量未观测关节。

A body model and learned motion prior fill in those degrees of freedom.
人体模型和运动先验，用来补足这些自由度。

DIP uses seventeen sensors to optimize reference poses, while sparse inference uses six.
DIP 用十七个传感器优化参考姿态，稀疏推理只用六个。

ProbIP models uncertainty in this body-tracking route. Its scores do not describe finger accuracy.
ProbIP 在这条全身动捕路线中建模不确定性，成绩不能套到手指上。

13 / 04:30  A glove is a form factor, not one sensor.

Gloves can use bend sensors, inertial sensing, electromagnetic tracking, or mechanical joint measurements.
手套可使用弯曲、惯性、电磁，或机械关节测量。

DexCap combines electromagnetic finger sensing with visual tracking for the wrist and scene.
DexCap 将手指电磁感知，与手腕及场景的视觉跟踪结合。

Fit and calibration matter. Metal can distort electromagnetic fields; a glove can slip.
贴合与标定很重要：金属会扰动电磁场，手套也可能滑移。

DexUMI instead uses a wearable mechanism to reduce human-to-robot kinematic differences.
DexUMI 则用可穿戴机构，减少人手与机器人之间的运动学差异。

14 / 04:54  UmeTrack: two different pipelines.

For virtual reality, let us trace UmeTrack from reference capture to runtime.
VR 部分，用 UmeTrack 从参考采集一路拆到实时推理。

Its dataset uses thirty-six external motion-capture cameras to track small markers on hands.
它的数据集利用三十六台外部动捕相机，跟踪手上的小标记。

A scanned hand profile and marker-based tracking provide the motion reference.
扫描得到的手部参数，与基于标记的跟踪共同提供动作参考。

The deployed visual tracker consumes headset images. The external rig belongs to reference collection.
运行时视觉跟踪器接收头显图像；外部装置用于采集参考。

15 / 05:16  1. Crop while preserving camera geometry.

Headset cameras have wide fields of view, and the hand moves across distorted image regions.
头显相机视野很广，人手会在不同畸变区域间移动。

UmeTrack forms perspective hand crops using calibrated camera geometry.
UmeTrack 利用标定的相机几何，生成手部透视裁剪。

This normalizes the region while preserving how each camera looks at the hand.
这一步规范输入区域，同时保留各相机观察人手的几何关系。

At runtime, a previous pose can guide tracking crops; initialization remains a separate requirement.
跟踪时可用上一帧姿态引导裁剪；初始化仍需要另外解决。

16 / 05:37  2. Put views into a common feature frame.

Each crop enters an image encoder. Camera geometry transforms the learned features into a common frame.
各裁剪进入图像编码器，相机几何再把特征变换到公共坐标系。

The fusion module combines available views, so one view can help with another view’s occlusion.
融合模块结合可用视角，用一个视角补充另一个视角的遮挡。

One-view and two-view tracking share the framework. Not every camera sees every hand.
单视角和双视角共享框架，并非每台相机都能看见每只手。

Its intermediate representation is learned features, rather than a measured depth point cloud.
中间表示是学习到的特征，而非实测深度点云。

17 / 06:00  3. Carry information across time.

A temporal module carries information from preceding frames into the current estimate.
时序模块把之前帧的信息，带入当前估计。

That history helps when observations are noisy or temporarily ambiguous.
历史信息可以帮助处理噪声和暂时的观测歧义。

A motion prior can suggest a hidden finger, but cannot create a new observation.
运动先验能够推测隐藏手指，却不能产生新的真实观测。

After an occlusion, compare recovery and delay, not just how smooth the animation looks.
遮挡结束后，要检查恢复与延迟，不能只看动画是否平滑。

18 / 06:20  4. Predict pose, then articulate the surface.

When a hand skeleton is known, its joint locations and axes condition pose prediction.
手部骨架已知时，关节位置与旋转轴作为姿态预测的条件。

It predicts twenty joint angles and a six-degree-of-freedom root transform.
它预测二十个关节角，以及六自由度的手根变换。

Forward kinematics places the joints. Skinning then moves the mesh with the bones.
正向运动学定位关节，蒙皮再让网格随骨骼运动。

Its unknown-skeleton calibration estimates overall scale; that is not a complete personalized surface scan.
未知骨架标定估计整体尺度，并不等于完整个体表面扫描。

19 / 06:42  Read the evaluation inputs before the score.

The official repository corrects earlier metrics and improves unknown-skeleton calibration.
官方仓库修正了早期指标，并改进未知骨架标定。

For known skeletons, it reports nine point four millimeters for separate hands.
已知骨架时，分离双手的平均关节误差为九点四毫米。

But the released evaluation uses ground-truth poses to generate the tracking crops.
但公开评测使用 GT 姿态生成跟踪裁剪区域。

This score therefore does not validate the complete detection and lost-track recovery pipeline.
因此这个成绩并未验证完整的检测与掉踪恢复流程。

20 / 07:03  A controlled comparison: add the second view.

HOT3D provides a more recent example of a controlled comparison using UmeTrack.
HOT3D 提供了较新的 UmeTrack 受控比较实例。

With joint training, the reported keypoint error drops from fifteen point four to ten point nine millimeters.
联合训练条件下，报告的关键点误差从十五点四降到十点九毫米。

The change is from one view to two, with ground-truth hand shape and boxes supplied.
改变的是单视角到双视角，同时提供 GT 手形与边界框。

Keep those conditions beside the numbers. They are not uncalibrated consumer-headset guarantees.
这些条件必须和数字放在一起，不能当作未标定消费头显的保证。

21 / 07:25  Small errors can answer different questions.

World-space error includes where the hand is. Wrist-relative error removes global translation.
世界空间误差包含手的位置；腕部相对误差去掉全局平移。

Procrustes alignment can additionally remove rotation and scale before measuring shape agreement.
Procrustes 对齐还能先去掉旋转与尺度，再比较形状一致性。

A reconstruction can score well after alignment while placing the robot’s fingers incorrectly.
重建在对齐后可能分数很好，机器人指尖落点却仍然错误。

Report the target, coordinate frame, alignment, and error distribution beside every score.
每个成绩旁边，都应写明目标量、坐标系、对齐方式与误差分布。

22 / 07:48  The frontier is a map of tasks.

For current research, separate reconstruction quality, tracking robustness, and reference-data generation.
看前沿研究时，要分开重建质量、跟踪鲁棒性和参考数据生成。

WiLoR and HaWoR address different visual outputs; SHOW3D advances capture in varied environments.
WiLoR 与 HaWoR 面向不同视觉输出；SHOW3D 推进多环境采集。

ProbIP studies sparse inertial uncertainty. DexCap and DexUMI optimize robot-data collection systems.
ProbIP 研究稀疏惯性不确定性；DexCap、DexUMI 优化机器人数据采集系统。

Call a method state of the art only for a named benchmark and matched protocol.
只有明确基准、对齐协议，才能讨论某个方法的 SOTA。

23 / 08:12  Look beyond the median.

SHOW3D’s manual-reference hand-object evaluation reports a median error of six point three six millimeters.
SHOW3D 对人工参考的手物交互评测，中位误差为六点三六毫米。

Its ninetieth percentile is sixteen point four eight millimeters.
但第九十百分位达到十六点四八毫米。

Both describe the same evaluation. A single typical value hides the difficult tail.
两者来自同一评测；只报典型值，会隐藏困难样本的尾部。

These evaluate annotation quality, not a real-time robot controller or every frame’s maximum error.
这些数字评价标注质量，并非实时控制器或每帧最大误差。

24 / 08:35  Smooth does not always mean responsive.

A filter can reduce jitter while delaying the motion you want to reproduce.
滤波能减少抖动，也可能推迟需要复现的动作。

Watch the peak: a smooth trajectory can still arrive too late.
看峰值的位置：轨迹很平滑，也可能来得太晚。

Processing frames per second is throughput. End-to-end latency includes capture, transfer, inference, and control.
每秒处理帧数是吞吐率；端到端延迟还包括采集、传输、推理与控制。

Measure delay, dropped tracking, and recovery separately from static precision.
延迟、掉踪与恢复，要和静态精度分别测量。

25 / 08:55  A good pose is only one part of teleoperation.

The robot has different link lengths, joint limits, and reachable contacts.
机器人的连杆长度、关节限位和可达接触，都与人手不同。

Retargeting must map the intended motion into that robot’s feasible configuration.
重定向需要把动作意图映射到机器人可执行的关节配置。

Geometric proximity does not establish contact force; force needs a separate sensing reference.
几何接近并不代表接触力；测力需要独立的传感参考。

Validate motion capture first, then mapping and closed-loop grasping, without combining their scores.
先验证动捕，再验证映射与闭环抓取，不把成绩混成一个数字。

26 / 09:18  Design a test that can prove the claim.

Use the same participants and motions, and synchronize each tracker with an independent reference.
固定受试者和动作，让各跟踪器与独立参考同步记录。

Include quick pinches, finger crossings, object occlusion, workspace edges, and repeated calibration.
包括快速捏合、手指交叉、物体遮挡、工作区边缘与重复标定。

Measure absolute wrist and fingertip error, relative pose, drift, dropout, and latency percentiles.
测量绝对腕部与指尖误差、相对姿态、漂移、掉踪及延迟分位数。

Publish missing-data coverage and evaluation inputs, including any ground-truth boxes or hand profiles.
公开缺失覆盖情况与评测输入，包括任何 GT 框或手部参数。

27 / 09:42  Choose the route for the missing information.

For a laboratory reference, prioritize calibrated coverage, synchronization, and independent validation.
实验室参考系统，优先保证标定覆盖、同步与独立验证。

For unencumbered hand interaction, compare calibrated multi-view vision against simpler camera baselines.
裸手交互，则把标定多视角视觉与更简单的相机基线比较。

For mobile or occluded capture, consider headset tracking or wearables, with their specific limitations.
移动或遮挡采集，可考虑头显跟踪或穿戴设备，并验证各自限制。

Fusion helps when sensors contribute complementary evidence; it also adds calibration work.
传感器提供互补证据时，融合才有帮助，同时也增加标定工作。

28 / 10:07  Trace the signal. Audit the reference.

Ground truth is a documented reference with a known construction process and measured limitations.
GT 是有来源、有构建过程，也有经过评估的局限性的参考。

Ask what was directly observed, what was fitted, and what independent evidence checks it.
追问哪些直接观测，哪些通过拟合，以及用什么独立证据验证。

Then compare methods under the same task and protocol, all the way to real contact.
再在相同任务与协议下比较方法，一路验证到真实接触。

Find the papers and learning map in DoF. Build the reference before trusting the score.
在 DoF 查看论文与学习地图：先建立参考，再相信分数。
