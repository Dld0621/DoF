DoF Paper EP.22 | Seeing Contact Before It Happens
指尖怎样在接触前后连续感知？

01 A coin meets a fingertip / 硬币遇到指尖
Standing a coin upright requires seeing a tiny edge before touching it and sensing contact afterward. FingerEye puts both observations near the fingertip, where wrist cameras can lose the most important local detail.
把硬币立起来，要先看清细边，再感知接触。FingerEye 把观测放到指尖附近，补足腕部视角。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=7

02 Two cameras inside the fingertip / 指尖里的双目相机
Two small RGB cameras observe local object geometry through a transparent contact structure. When the compliant structure deforms, its internal visual features also reveal changes associated with contact, creating a continuous sensing interface.
两枚 RGB 相机透过透明接触结构观察局部几何。柔顺结构变形时，内部视觉特征继续反映接触变化。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=3

03 Before and after contact / 接触前后接起来
Before contact, the useful signal is alignment with a rim, edge, or narrow opening. After contact, the same local views reveal deformation and changing contact geometry, helping the robot avoid relying only on distant appearance.
接触前，需要对齐边缘、细边或狭窄开口。接触后，局部视图继续提供形变与几何变化。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=2

04 Do not bury local information / 别淹没局部信息
The policy first encodes observation groups separately, including each fingertip, wrist vision, and proprioception. Group-conditioned decoding then gives every group a direct influence on action prediction, reducing competition with easier global visual cues.
策略先分别编码指尖、腕部视觉和本体信息。解码时各组直接影响动作，减少全局线索的压制。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=19

05 What the learned policy reads / 学习策略实际读取什么
The real setup uses an xArm7 and a LEAP hand with matched leader-follower demonstrations. The main learned policy reads images and robot state, not estimated contact wrench or plate-pose values directly.
真机是 xArm7 与 LEAP 手，通过同构主从采示教。主策略读取图像与状态，不直接输入估计力或板位姿。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=5

06 Four physical tasks / 四项真机任务
Twenty trials per task test coin standing, chip grasping, envelope handling, and syringe operation. The four-task average is seventy-one point two five percent, compared with thirty-seven point five using wrist vision alone.
四项真机任务各二十次，覆盖硬币、薯片、信件和注射器。平均成功率七十一点二五，腕部视觉为三十七点五。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=7

07 What the comparison supports / 比较能支持什么
That thirty-three point seven five percentage-point improvement comes from a matched sensing ablation. It supports the value of local continuous observations in this setup, not a universal ranking against every complete dexterous robot system.
提高三十三点七五个百分点，来自同设置模态消融。支持局部连续观测的价值，不代表所有灵巧系统排名。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=7

08 Separate the fragile-object demo / 单独看脆弱物体演示
The striking fragile-object grasp demonstrations use predefined motion and object-specific stopping thresholds. They are separate from the learned-policy benchmark, and three additional simulated tasks must not be counted as physical experiments.
脆弱物体演示采用预设轨迹和物体专用停止阈值。它不同于学习策略测试，另三项仿真也不能算真机。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=4

09 Design sensing around the task / 围绕任务设计感知
FingerEye illustrates why sensor placement can change what a policy is able to learn. For deployment, evaluate local visibility, calibration, sensor geometry, and training data together instead of assuming more cameras automatically produce better contact control.
FingerEye 展示感知位置如何改变策略能学到什么。落地还要同时验证可见性、标定、几何和训练数据。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.20689v4#page=2
