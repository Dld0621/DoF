DoF Paper EP.30 | UVTA | Learn the Contact Behind an Action
UVTA：从人类触觉学习接触规律

01 Turn exactly one page / 翻过去，还要只翻一页
Turning a page requires more than moving a fingertip through the right trajectory. The robot must separate the paper and regulate contact without picking up several sheets.
翻页不只是让指尖经过正确轨迹。机器人还要分离纸页，避免同时带起多页。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Figure 1 | interaction representation, PDF p1

02 Collect the missing signal / 把人类触觉一起录下来
UVTA records synchronized images, tactile signals, wrist pose, and hand motion during human demonstrations. A tactile motion-capture apparatus makes the contact evidence available alongside the visible action.
UVTA同步记录人类图像、触觉、腕姿和手部运动。触觉动捕装置让可见动作拥有对应接触证据。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Figure 2 | synchronized tactile capture, PDF p3

03 Align two embodiments / 对齐人手和机器人
Human and robot sensing enter an aligned visual-tactile interaction representation. The purpose is to share useful interaction structure while retaining actions suitable for each embodiment.
人类和机器人感知进入对齐的视触觉交互表征。目标是共享交互规律，同时保留各手型适用的动作。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Figure 4 | joint prediction, PDF p4

04 Predict action and touch / 联合预测动作与触觉
The model jointly predicts future action trajectories and future tactile trajectories during training. This auxiliary tactile objective encourages the representation to encode what an action should feel like.
训练时，模型联合预测未来动作轨迹和触觉轨迹。辅助触觉目标促使表征理解动作应产生怎样的接触。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Figure 4 | joint prediction, PDF p4

05 Training and deployment differ / 训练目标不等于控制输出
Future tactile prediction helps train the model; it is not a separate deployed controller output. At execution, the robot carries out robot actions and receives new sensory observations.
未来触觉预测用于训练，并非独立部署控制输出。执行时机器人输出动作，并接收新的感知观测。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Figure 4 | joint prediction, PDF p4

06 The experimental budget / 数据量与试验次数
Each of five tasks uses one hundred fifty robot and one thousand human demonstrations. Each method is evaluated ten times per task on the physical robot.
五项任务各150条机器人、1000条人类示范。每种方法每任务进行10次真机评测。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Figure 2 | synchronized tactile capture, PDF p3

07 Seventy percent of what? / 70%究竟是什么？
The reported average is seventy percent, compared with twenty-nine for the retrained T-Rex baseline. Here, the metric awards partial credit for completed stages rather than counting only complete tasks.
报告平均70%，对应重训T-Rex基线29%。这里按完成阶段给部分分数，并非只数完整成功。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Table 1 | phase-weighted scoring, PDF p7

08 Read the page-turning score / 用翻页看懂阶段计分
Separating a page earns point three; flipping multiple pages earns point seven. Only correctly flipping a single page earns one, so an average score is not full success.
分离纸页得0.3分，翻多页得0.7分。正确翻一页才得1分，平均得分不等于完整成功率。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Evaluation scoring rules | PDF p6

09 What human touch contributes / 人类触觉带来了什么
Removing future tactile prediction reduces the average score to forty-two under this protocol. The evidence supports contact-aware representation learning, with small evaluation counts and explicitly partial-credit outcomes.
移除未来触觉预测，同协议平均分降到42。证据支持接触表征学习，但试验少且需保留计分定义。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34182v2; Table 1 | phase-weighted scoring, PDF p7
