DoF Paper EP.15 | Learning a Contact Model in Minutes
两分钟学会接触模型？

01 Rotate and stop / 转过去，还要停得准
Spinning a cube is easier than stopping it at a requested orientation. ContactSDF learns a compact contact model, then uses that model to plan controlled rotation on an Allegro hand.
让方块一直转，比转到目标角度更容易。ContactSDF 学习接触模型，规划掌上受控旋转。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=1

02 Two distance functions / 两种距离函数
The first signed distance function describes geometry: how close the bodies are to contact. A second function works in a transformed velocity space, approximating how contact changes the next motion.
第一个有符号距离函数描述物体离接触多近。第二个作用于变换后的速度空间，预测接触运动。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=3

03 A model that can be optimized / 能用于优化的模型
Together they replace expensive contact calculations with a smooth, differentiable prediction of the next state. This makes the model useful inside an optimizer, rather than merely reconstructing the visible surface of an object.
两者形成平滑、可微的下一状态预测。用途是支持控制优化，而不只是重建可见表面。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=1

04 Learn while controlling / 边控制，边学模型
Model predictive control proposes finger movements, and the robot executes them through a low-level controller. Observed state transitions then update the contact model, closing a loop between prediction, action, and measured outcomes.
MPC 提议手指动作，底层控制器负责执行。观测到的状态转移再更新模型，形成闭环。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=5

05 What the real robot needs / 真机需要哪些输入
Hardware experiments use a fifty-six-millimeter marked cube and an Allegro V4 hand. A depth camera, AprilTags, and TagSLAM provide object pose; this external tracking remains part of the deployed system.
实验使用五十六毫米带标记方块和 Allegro 手。相机、AprilTag 和 TagSLAM 持续提供物体位姿。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=7

06 Data efficiency, carefully read / 怎样理解数据效率
The model adapts using about two thousand real hardware steps in under two minutes. These are state-transition measurements for model fitting, not human action labels, and they assume usable geometry and pose estimates.
模型用不到两分钟、约两千个真机步适应。监督来自状态转移，同时依赖几何和位姿估计。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=5

07 The physical rotation result / 真实旋转结果
For positive ninety-degree rotations, the mean final error is about six point three degrees. The negative direction averages ten point nine degrees; each direction uses five trials, so uncertainty matters.
正向九十度旋转，平均角误差约六点三度。反向约十点九度，各五次试验，波动也要看。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=7

08 Do not change the metric / 别偷换精度指标
Those numbers measure the cube relative to its target orientation, not camera tracking accuracy. They also do not establish millimeter assembly precision, arbitrary-object generalization, or performance without external pose tracking.
这些数字测方块相对目标的角误差。并非相机精度，也未证明任意物体装配能力。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=7

09 The engineering takeaway / 工程上学到什么
ContactSDF shows a useful middle ground between a fixed physics model and a large learned policy. Learn a small contact model from interaction, but validate its approximations and sensing dependencies for each intended task.
它介于固定物理模型和大规模学习策略之间。用交互学习小模型，同时验证近似与感知依赖。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.09612v2#page=5
