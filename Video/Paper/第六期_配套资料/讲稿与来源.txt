DoF Paper EP.06 | TeleDexter: Track the Object, Not Only the Hand
TeleDexter：不仅跟手，还要跟住物体

01 A matching pose can still lose contact / 姿态相似，仍可能失去接触
Copying a person's finger motion can fail when robot contacts behave differently. TeleDexter tracks both hand targets and the object's desired movement during teleoperation. A learned controller decides how to maintain useful contact while following them.
机器人接触不同，照抄人手运动也可能失败。TeleDexter在遥操中同时跟踪手和物体目标。学习控制器决定如何边跟踪边保持有效接触。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 7

02 Prepare physically useful references / 先准备物理上有用的参考
Motion capture provides hand-object trajectories, but raw references may contain incompatible geometry. Reference optimization adjusts contact, penetration, self-collision, and temporal smoothness before policy training. The output is a sequence of fingertip and object-pose subgoals.
动捕提供手物轨迹，原始参考可能几何不相容。参考优化先修正接触、穿透、自碰撞与平滑性。输出是一串指尖与物体位姿子目标。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 4

03 Learn to move between subgoals / 学习如何连接子目标
A simulation-trained controller learns contact transitions rather than rigidly copying every reference frame. Randomization and action masking help transfer that behavior to the physical hand. This adds dynamics-aware control beyond a purely geometric fingertip mapping.
仿真控制器学习接触切换，而非逐帧僵硬照搬。随机化和动作遮蔽帮助迁移到真实手。这在纯几何指尖映射之外加入动力学控制。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 4

04 What the deployed system actually reads / 部署系统实际读取什么
The real system uses NOKOV tracking for human motion and marked object poses. The operator switches from geometric pre-grasp mapping to the learned contact controller. Neither the object-pose input nor this human switching should disappear from the explanation.
真机用NOKOV追踪人手与带标记物体姿态。操作者从预抓几何映射切换到学习接触控制器。物体位姿输入和人工切换都不能从解释中省略。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 7

05 Substantial object-specific preparation / 每个物体都需要准备
Training uses one hundred fifty twenty-second reference clips for each object. Controllers also require extensive simulated interaction and task-specific object information. Supporting two robot hands demonstrates adaptation of the workflow, not universal shared weights.
每个物体使用150段、每段20秒参考。控制器还需要大量仿真交互与物体信息。支持两种手证明流程可适配，不是通用同一权重。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 4

06 Success and progress are different columns / 成功与进度是两列指标
Across seven SharpaWave tasks, complete success averages seventy-five point two percent. Task progress averages eighty-seven point one percent because partial completion also receives credit. Each method receives fifteen trials per task under the same reported protocol.
七项SharpaWave任务完整成功率平均75.2%。阶段进度平均87.1%，因为部分完成也计分。每个方法按同协议，每任务测试15次。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 7

07 Autonomous skills are a separate experiment / 自主技能是另一组实验
A separate diffusion policy is trained from newly collected demonstrations for three autonomous tasks. Those results must not be merged with the stronger human-in-the-loop teleoperation score. New objects still require dedicated references and additional controller training.
另采示教训练扩散策略，测试三项自主任务。这些结果不能混入更高的人在环遥操分数。新物体仍需专门参考和额外控制器训练。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 8

08 The practical tradeoff / 实际需要权衡什么
TeleDexter makes contact dynamics part of the mapping from human intention to robot action. Its gain comes with external tracking and substantial object-specific preparation. Choose it for controlled contact-rich teleoperation, without assuming cheap markerless deployment.
它把接触动力学纳入人意图到机器人动作的映射。收益伴随外部跟踪和大量物体专用准备。它适合受控接触遥操，不能假定低成本无标记部署。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.11481v1; PDF page 7
