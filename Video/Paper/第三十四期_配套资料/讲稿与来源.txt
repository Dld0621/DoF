DoF Paper EP.34 | TactiDex | Transfer Contact, Not Just Hand Motion
TactiDex：让机器人学到接触，而不只是手形

01 The motion looks convincing / 动作像，接触未必对
A robot can imitate a hand trajectory while missing the contact that actually makes the task work. TactiDex records human touch alongside motion, and TactiSkill uses that information to guide transfer.
机器人能模仿手轨迹，却错过真正起作用的接触。TactiDex同步采集触觉与运动，TactiSkill用这些信息指导迁移。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Figure 1 | human motion and touch capture, PDF p3

02 Synchronize human interaction / 同步记录人类交互
The dataset aligns whole-hand tactile measurements with hand motion and object states. Post-optimization and sensor-to-simulation mapping turn these recordings into physically aligned references for training.
数据集对齐全手触觉、手部运动与物体状态。后优化和传感器到仿真的映射生成物理对齐参考。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Figure 1 | human motion and touch capture, PDF p3

03 Reward one: establish contact / 第一步：建立该有的接触
The transfer framework trains a residual tracking policy with three complementary tactile reward components. The first encourages the robot to establish contact where the human demonstration indicates it matters.
迁移框架用三种互补触觉奖励训练残差跟踪策略。第一项引导机器人在示范的重要区域建立接触。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Figure 2 | tactile transfer pipeline, PDF p5

04 Reward two: align interaction / 第二步：对齐接触模式
A human-like alignment term encourages the transferred interaction to follow the recorded contact and force pattern. Matching visible joint motion alone would leave this part of the physical behavior unconstrained.
类人对齐项使迁移交互遵循记录的接触和力模式。只匹配可见关节运动，无法约束这部分物理行为。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Figure 2 | tactile transfer pipeline, PDF p5

05 Reward three: constrain force / 第三步：约束接触力
A contact-constraint term limits problematic forces while the policy adjusts the retargeted action. The intended result is a motion that preserves useful interaction, rather than merely resembling a pose sequence.
接触约束项限制不合理力，策略则调整重定向动作。目标是保留有效交互，而不只是姿态序列相似。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Figure 2 | tactile transfer pipeline, PDF p5

06 The main quantitative setting / 主要定量结果来自哪里
The main comparison uses seventy-three reference sequences evaluated in simulation. The tactile-fidelity criterion measures whether the transferred contact matches the reference under its specified rules.
主要比较使用73条参考序列，在仿真中评测。触觉保真判据衡量迁移接触是否满足参考匹配规则。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Table 2 | simulation fidelity, PDF p7

07 Read the fidelity result / 读懂触觉保真结果
Tactile-fidelity success reaches sixty-four point six four percent, versus thirty-nine point three five for kinematic transfer. Contact F-one rises from point five five six nine to point seven three eight four.
触觉保真64.64%，运动学迁移为39.35%。Contact F1由0.5569升到0.7384。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Table 2 | simulation fidelity, PDF p7

08 The real robot is open loop / 真机没有在线触觉闭环
The appendix states that physical deployment does not feed tactile signals back into the policy online. The robot performs open-loop execution, so these demonstrations cannot establish autonomous tactile-feedback reliability.
附录明确：真机执行不把触觉在线反馈给策略。真机采用开环执行，不能证明自主触觉反馈可靠性。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Appendix C.3 | real execution is open loop, PDF p14

09 A contact-supervision resource / 一套接触监督资源
TactiDex contributes measured human interaction; TactiSkill demonstrates how that contact can shape a transfer objective. Keep simulation fidelity, kinematic matching, and complete real-world task success as separate questions.
TactiDex提供人类交互测量，TactiSkill展示接触如何塑造迁移目标。仿真保真、运动匹配、真机完整成功应分别评价。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.09190v1; Figure 1 | human motion and touch capture, PDF p3
