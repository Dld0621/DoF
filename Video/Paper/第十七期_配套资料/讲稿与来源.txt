DoF Paper EP.17 | A Robot in an Unfamiliar Home
换一个家，机器人还会干活吗？

01 Beyond a familiar tabletop / 走出熟悉的实验台
A robot enters a new home and must put dishes away or collect laundry. Pi zero point five studies this environmental generalization problem, where objects, layouts, and intermediate steps change together.
机器人进入陌生家庭，要收拾碗碟或衣物。π0.5 研究物体、布局与步骤同时变化的泛化。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=1

02 Decide the next step / 先决定下一步
A high-level prediction identifies the next semantic subtask from the current observations and instruction. For example, the robot may need to open a drawer before placing the object inside it.
高层根据当前观测和指令，选择下一语义子任务。例如放入物体之前，要先把抽屉打开。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=4

03 Turn intent into motion / 再把意图变成动作
A continuous action expert then generates the movements needed to execute that subtask. Flow matching supplies action sequences for the mobile base, two arms, and grippers, conditioned on current robot observations.
连续动作专家生成执行该子任务的运动。流匹配根据观测，为底盘、双臂和夹爪生成动作。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=4

04 Learn from different kinds of data / 融合不同类型的数据
The training recipe mixes robot actions, semantic task annotations, and visual-language supervision. Discrete action training and continuous control share useful representations, connecting web-scale understanding with the physical behaviors learned from robot demonstrations.
训练混合机器人动作、语义标注与视觉语言任务。离散动作训练与连续控制共享表示，连接理解和行为。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=6

05 Training is not deployment / 训练信息不等于部署输入
At deployment, the policy uses camera views, robot proprioception, and language. The human high-level oracle is a comparison condition, not an extra operator silently choosing every subtask for the autonomous policy.
部署使用相机、本体状态和语言。人工高层指令是对照条件，不是自主策略的隐藏输入。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=1

06 What the home tests measure / 家庭测试究竟测什么
The main real-home plots report task progress across previously unseen environments. Each task and environment receives ten trials, but the vertical axis counts completed stages rather than only entirely successful episodes.
真实家庭主图统计未见环境中的任务进度。每任务每环境十次，纵轴按完成阶段计分。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=8

07 Progress is not full completion / 进度不等于全部完成
For four dishes, picking each one up and placing it down can earn separate points. A high progress score can therefore include unfinished episodes; it should not be presented as the full-task success probability.
四件碗碟的拿起和放下，可以分别得分。高进度也可能包含未完成试验，不能替换成成功率。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=17

08 Where generalization still fails / 泛化仍会在哪里失败
Unfamiliar handles, difficult doors, arm occlusion, and repetitive behaviors still cause failures. These mobile-gripper demonstrations also do not measure fingertip slip control, multi-finger coordination, or tight-tolerance assembly accuracy.
陌生把手、难开的门、遮挡与重复动作仍会失败。移动夹爪实验未测多指滑移控制或紧公差装配。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=1

09 Two separate capability axes / 两条不同的能力轴
Pi zero point five expands where a robot can attempt useful, extended tasks. For dexterous manipulation, environmental generalization and precise contact control must still be tested separately before claiming dependable everyday operation.
π0.5 扩展了机器人尝试长任务的环境范围。环境泛化和精密接触仍需分别验证可靠性。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.16054v1#page=6
