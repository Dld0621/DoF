把人手意图变成机器人捏取

Different hands, the same intended pinch / 手的结构不同，捏合意图相同
Part two follows one intention from the tracked hand into the robot: pinch a small object. The human model has five fingers; Allegro has four, with different joint axes and locations. Copying each human joint angle therefore does not preserve where the robot fingertips meet. DexPilot asks robot fingertips to reproduce spatial relationships that matter for grasping. This changes retargeting from matching anatomy into optimizing useful geometry.
中篇沿着跟踪到的人手信息进入机器人，先看一个简单意图：捏住小物体。人手模型有五根手指，Allegro只有四根，关节轴和位置也不同。因此，逐个照抄人手关节角，无法保持机器人指尖的会合位置。DexPilot转而要求机器人指尖复现对抓握重要的空间关系。这样，重定向就从匹配解剖结构，变为优化有用的几何关系。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A, PDF pp.5–7; Fig.9, PDF p.7

Ten vectors make the hand relationship visible / 用十条向量表示手内关系
In Figure eight, four arrows connect the palm to four represented fingertips. Three more run from the index, middle, and ring fingertips toward the thumb. Three connect pairs among those primary fingers, giving ten illustrated vectors. The human little finger has no matching fingertip in this set. Palm vectors retain the overall arrangement, while fingertip vectors describe closing gaps and coordinating a grasp.
数图8里的箭头：四条从掌心连接到四个参与映射的指尖。另外三条从食指、中指和无名指指尖指向拇指。余下三条连接这三根主指之间的两两组合，图中合计十条向量。图示向量集中，人手小指没有对应的机器人指尖。掌心向量保留整体布局，指尖间向量描述间隙闭合和抓握协同。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Fig.8 and Table I, PDF p.7; vector count is a visual count, not a numerical N stated in the prose

A vector carries direction as well as distance / 向量同时保留方向与距离
Each vector points between two coordinate origins and is expressed in the coordinate frame where it starts. Its length measures separation, while its components also depend on the orientation of that local frame. Matching only distances would discard these directional relationships and allow more geometrically different solutions. Corresponding human and robot frames must therefore have similar placement and orientation. Frame conventions are part of the method, not decorative axes on the illustration.
每条向量连接两个坐标原点，并用起点所在坐标系表示。长度表示间距，而向量各分量还取决于局部坐标系的朝向。如果只匹配距离，就会丢掉这些方向关系，允许更多几何形态不同的解。因此，论文要求人手和机器人模型的对应坐标系具有相近的位置与朝向。坐标约定是方法的一部分，图里的坐标轴并非装饰。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A, PDF pp.5,7

What the optimizer actually minimizes / 优化器究竟在最小化什么
Hold the tracked human joint angles fixed, and search over the Allegro joint angles. For every selected vector, compare the robot vector with a human direction multiplied by a chosen target length. Square that three-dimensional error, multiply it by a switching weight, and sum the contributions with a one-half factor. A separate penalty on the robot joint angles favors a more open hand when other objectives permit it. There is no object mesh, contact force target, or human-to-robot joint-angle difference in this objective.
固定跟踪得到的人手关节角，搜索Allegro的关节角。对每条选定向量，将机器人向量与“人手方向乘以目标长度”比较。把三维误差平方，乘上切换权重，再求和并乘二分之一。另一项对机器人关节角的惩罚，在其他目标允许时偏向更张开的手型。这个目标里没有物体网格、接触力目标，也没有人手机器人关节角之差。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A, cost equation, PDF p.5; regularizer explanation, PDF p.7

Away from a pinch, scale the geometry / 未进入捏合区时，缩放几何关系
First consider a human fingertip separation larger than the threshold called epsilon. The weight is one, and the target length is the measured length multiplied by one point six. The normalized human vector supplies the direction, so this branch scales the vector without rotating it. This accommodates different hand geometry while retaining an interpretable mapping. But simply scaling a small tracking gap would preserve a gap precisely when a pinch needs closure.
先看人手指尖间距大于阈值epsilon的情况。此时权重为1，目标长度等于测得长度乘以1.6。归一化的人手向量提供方向，因此这一分支只缩放向量，不改变方向。这样可以适应手部几何差异，同时保持与操作者动作之间可理解的对应。但如果把很小的跟踪间隙直接缩放，恰好需要捏合时，间隙仍会存在。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A, switching and distancing functions, PDF p.6

Primary projection favors closing toward the thumb / 第一类投影：优先向拇指闭合
The first projected set contains vectors from each primary finger toward the thumb. When the applicable separation is at or below epsilon, its weight rises from one to two hundred. Its target length becomes one ten-thousandth of a meter, which is one tenth of a millimeter. That is a very small nonzero geometric target, rather than an exact zero-distance constraint. The optimizer prioritizes closing that relationship even if lower-weight vectors cannot match perfectly.
第一类投影向量从各根主指指向拇指。当对应间距小于等于epsilon时，权重从1提高到200。目标长度变为万分之一米，也就是0.1毫米。这是一个很小但非零的几何目标，并非严格零距离约束。优化器此时优先闭合这组指尖关系，即使其他低权重向量无法完美匹配。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Table I, PDF p.7; s and f equations, PDF p.6

Secondary projection keeps neighboring fingers apart / 第二类投影：保留相邻主指间距
Closing multiple primary fingers toward the same thumb creates another problem: those fingers may crowd each other. The second set contains vectors between primary fingers when both are being projected with the thumb. For the projected branch, the specified target separation is three centimeters, with a weight of four hundred. These priorities encourage precision grasping while discouraging the primary fingers from collapsing together. The printed formulation is a weighted geometric penalty, so the three-centimeter target is not a proven hard collision constraint.
多根主指同时向拇指闭合，会带来另一个问题：它们可能互相挤在一起。当两根主指都向拇指投影时，它们之间的向量属于第二类集合。在投影分支中，指定目标间距为3厘米，权重为400。这些更高优先级促进精细抓握，同时抑制主指相互塌缩到一起。印出的公式是加权几何惩罚，所以3厘米目标并不是已证明的硬碰撞约束。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Table I and explanation, PDF p.7; s and f equations, PDF p.6

What the paper specifies, and what it leaves open / 论文公开了什么，哪些仍需补充
The printed equations switch branches using a distance threshold, together with the primary and secondary set definitions. However, this version does not publish a numerical value for epsilon. It also does not specify separate activation and release thresholds or a hysteresis state machine. The appendix says that the projection threshold can require tuning when grasping small objects. Exact reproduction needs those details; importing thresholds from later software would change the evidence.
印出的公式结合距离阈值，以及两类集合的定义，切换不同分支。但这一版本没有公开epsilon的具体数值。它也没有写出独立的开启、释放阈值，或滞回状态机。附录说明，抓取小物体时可能需要调节投影阈值。因此精确复现仍需这些实现细节；套用后来软件中的常见阈值，会改变证据来源。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A, PDF pp.5–7; Appendix XII-E, PDF p.17; absence checked throughout v2

An illustrative pinch on one small object / 用同一个小物体解释捏合过程
Imagine pinching one small cube; this is an illustration, not an additional experiment. While the fingers remain separated, the optimizer follows the scaled human vectors and the robot approaches the object. As the thumb and index relationship enters the projection branch, its desired gap shrinks and its priority increases. The physical cube obstructs further closure, but the geometric objective alone does not determine the resulting contact force. When releasing that same cube, projection can delay opening, which explains why a helpful pinch mechanism can also hinder precise placement.
设想操作者引导机器人捏住一个小方块；这段过程是解释性示意，并非新增实验。手指尚未靠拢时，优化器跟随缩放后的人手向量，机器人接近物体。当拇指与食指关系进入投影分支时，目标间隙缩小，优先级提高。真实方块会阻挡继续闭合，但几何目标本身无法决定最终接触力。释放同一个方块时，投影可能拖延张开，这也解释了有利于捏合的机制为何会妨碍精确放置。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Explanatory construction from Section VII-A, PDF pp.5–7; release limitation Section X, PDF p.10; Fig.18, PDF p.12

The regularizer prefers an open configuration / 正则项偏向张开构型
Different robot joint configurations can produce similar fingertip relationships, leaving redundant solutions. DexPilot adds the squared magnitude of the Allegro joint angles, weighted by zero point zero zero two five. Zero angles correspond to the fully open hand in this model. The authors report that this preference helped avoid problematic minima, such as fingers curling into the palm. This term penalizes distance from zero, not change from the previous frame; temporal smoothing is handled separately.
不同的机器人关节构型，可能产生相近的指尖关系，因此解存在冗余。DexPilot加入Allegro关节角的平方范数，权重为0.0025。在这个模型里，零角度对应完全张开的手。作者报告，这种偏好有助于避开手指卷入掌心等不良局部极小值。这一项惩罚偏离零角度，而不是与上一帧的差值；时间平滑另行处理。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A, PDF p.7

Constraints and a warm-started numerical search / 用约束和上一帧解推进数值搜索
The distal joint of each primary robot finger is constrained to equal its medial joint. This reduces redundancy and search space while encouraging humanlike motion. The authors solve the objective with sequential least-squares quadratic programming from the NLopt library. The first solve starts at zero; later solves start from the previous solution. Forward kinematics tests how changing candidate robot joints changes the vectors.
机器人每根主指的远端关节，被约束为等于其中间关节。这减少了冗余与搜索空间，并用关节耦合形成接近人手的运动。作者使用NLopt库中的序列最小二乘二次规划方法求解目标。第一次求解从机器人零角度开始，之后每次都从上一帧解开始。正向运动学提供候选机器人向量，因此每次数值更新都能评估关节变化如何改变几何关系。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A implementation, PDF p.7

Smoothing cannot recover missing contact information / 平滑不能补回缺失的接触信息
A first-order low-pass filter is applied after optimization to reduce tracking noise in the retargeted angles. It also smooths the abrupt changes introduced when projection switches branches. The paper does not report the filter coefficient, so an exact numerical replay requires another implementation detail. The appendix also reports an attempted neural retargeter whose accuracy was unsatisfactory compared with online optimization. Without quantitative ablations, this is an author observation rather than a universal ranking.
优化之后，再用一阶低通滤波器降低重定向角度中的跟踪噪声。它还会平滑投影分支切换引入的突变。论文未报告滤波系数，因此精确数值复现还需要这一实现细节。附录还报告了神经网络重定向尝试，其精度相对于在线优化并不令人满意。那里没有提供定量重定向消融，因此这属于作者观察，而非测得的普遍性能排名。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-A, PDF p.7; Appendix XII-E, PDF p.17

RMPs move the palm through the workspace / RMP负责让手掌在空间中运动
Finger retargeting handles the grasp configuration; a separate motion generator handles the robot palm pose. Riemannian Motion Policies combine acceleration fields for pose following and collision avoidance. Collision planes help keep the arm and palm away from the table and the operator. An initial open-hand calibration aligns camera and robot coordinates, with both palms parallel to the table and fingers forward. The resulting arm trajectories go to a torque-level impedance controller, which executes the generated motion on the physical arm.
手指重定向处理抓握构型，独立的运动生成器处理机器人手掌位姿。黎曼运动策略将位姿跟随与碰撞规避对应的加速度场组合起来。碰撞平面帮助机械臂和手掌避开桌面与操作者。初始张手标定对齐相机与机器人坐标，两只手掌都平行于桌面、手指朝前。生成的机械臂轨迹交给力矩级阻抗控制器，在真实机械臂上执行。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-B, PDF p.7; Fig.4, PDF p.4

Update rate is not end-to-end responsiveness / 更新频率不等于端到端响应速度
The paper sends target arm trajectories to the arm controller at two hundred hertz. It sends retargeted Allegro angles to the hand joint controller at thirty hertz. These are command update rates, not a statement that the complete vision-to-motion delay is five milliseconds. The architecture section reports approximately one second of overall latency across the chained components. The pilot must therefore judge and correct a delayed physical interaction, which makes the experimental protocol essential to interpreting the results.
论文以200赫兹向机械臂控制器发送目标轨迹。以30赫兹向手部关节控制器发送重定向后的Allegro角度。这些是指令更新频率，并不表示从视觉到运动的完整延迟只有5毫秒。系统架构部分报告，串联各模块后的总体延迟约为1秒。因此操作者必须判断并修正存在延迟的物理交互，这让实验协议成为理解结果的关键。
Source: Handa et al. · DexPilot (2019), v2 · Section VII-B, PDF p.7; Section IV, PDF p.3
