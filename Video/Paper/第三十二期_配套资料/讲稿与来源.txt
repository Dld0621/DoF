DoF Paper EP.32 | FoLD | Copy the Function, Correct the Contact
FoLD：模仿手形，为什么没推动物体？

01 The hand looks right / 手形像了，物体却没动
A robot copies a person closing a laptop, yet the screen follows the wrong motion. FoLD uses the object-motion error to guide corrections to the robot fingers.
机器人模仿人关电脑，屏幕却没有按预期运动。FoLD利用物体运动误差指导机器人手指修正。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; fixed PDF page 1. Full Figure 1 crop including all visual rows and labels, without body text.

02 Start from human interaction / 从人类手物交互开始
Human demonstrations provide hand trajectories and the desired motion of an articulated object. An imitator retargets the motion, supplying a baseline that a residual policy can adjust.
人类示范提供手轨迹和铰接物体的期望运动。模仿器重定向运动，残差策略在此基础上调整。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; Figure 2 | compensatory force field, PDF p4

03 Estimate a compensatory field / 估计补偿力场
When the object deviates, the method estimates a virtual compensatory force field. Current interaction state and contact structure describe what correction would better reproduce the demonstrated motion.
物体偏离参考时，方法估计虚拟补偿力场。当前交互状态与接触结构描述所需运动修正。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; Figure 2 | compensatory force field, PDF p4

04 Use force as information / 力场作为信息输入
An encoder compresses this field into information for the residual policy. During evaluation, it does not secretly push the object with an external corrective force.
编码器把力场压缩为残差策略的输入信息。评测时不会用外部补偿力直接推动物体。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; Figure 2 | compensatory force field, PDF p4

05 Correct robot actions / 修正机器人的动作
The policy changes the robot action so its own contacts can produce the desired object behavior. This requires known models, interaction states, and task-specific training within the simulated setup.
策略改变机器人动作，由自身接触实现目标物体运动。这依赖已知模型、交互状态和仿真中的任务专门训练。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; Figure 2 | compensatory force field, PDF p4

06 What the main score measures / 主指标衡量完成到哪里
The main reported success measure is completed trajectory length divided by reference length. An attempt is truncated when positional error exceeds fifteen centimeters or rotation error exceeds forty degrees.
主表所谓成功指标，是完成轨迹长度除以参考长度。位置超过15厘米或转角超过40度时截断。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; L/T completion definition | PDF p6

07 A paired simulation comparison / 配对仿真结果
Across twenty-seven paired hand-task units, completion ratio is point seven seven two versus point six zero six. Average articulation error falls from thirty-one point zero six to fourteen point eight three degrees.
27个配对手型任务单元，完成比例0.772对0.606。平均铰接误差从31.06度降到14.83度。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; Table I | paired simulation comparison, PDF p6

08 Real demonstration is open loop / 真机演示采用开环回放
The physical laptop-closing and headphone-folding demonstrations replay trajectories without autonomous sensory feedback. They support execution feasibility, not validated closed-loop real-world task success.
真机关电脑和折耳机采用无自主感知反馈的轨迹回放。它们支持执行可行性，尚不能证明真机闭环成功。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33551v1; Figure 7 | real open-loop replay, PDF p8

09 Judge the object, not the pose / 评价物体效果，不只看手形
FoLD shifts attention from matching hand geometry to producing the intended object motion. Results are not uniformly better across hands, so inspect the per-embodiment rows before generalizing.
FoLD把关注点从手形相似转向物体运动效果。并非所有手型都更优，结论要保留逐手型差异。
