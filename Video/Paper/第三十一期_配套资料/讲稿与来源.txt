DoF Paper EP.31 | SoTa | Give Human and Robot Hands the Same Touch Map
SoTa：让人手与机器人共享一张触觉地图

01 One cup, not the stack / 只拿一只杯子
A hand lifts a thin cup, but accidentally brings the nested cups along. SoTa explores how full-hand tactile sensing and human demonstrations can improve this kind of interaction.
手拿起薄杯，却把套在一起的杯子都带起来。SoTa研究全手触觉和人类示范怎样改善这种交互。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; fixed PDF page 1. Full Figure 1 crop including all visual rows and labels, without body text.

02 A shared sensor layout / 两种手，同样的触觉布局
The soft capacitive skins place two hundred two sensing elements across corresponding finger and palm regions. Human and robot skins fit different shapes while preserving this shared layout.
柔性电容皮肤在对应手指和手掌区域布置202个单元。人手和机器人皮肤适配不同外形，但保持共享布局。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; Figures 2 and 3 | skin and collection, PDF p4

03 Fabric, electrodes, contact / 传感皮肤如何感受接触
Layered fabric electrodes create a thin, deformable sensor that responds to contact. The paper reports material cost below ten dollars per skin, not the price of a complete system.
多层织物电极形成能响应接触的薄柔性传感器。每片材料成本低于10美元，并非整套系统售价。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; Figures 2 and 3 | skin and collection, PDF p4

04 Share the tactile encoder / 共享触觉编码器
Corresponding sensing layouts allow both embodiments to use a common tactile encoder. This avoids learning a separate cross-sensor conversion just to relate human contact to robot contact.
对应传感布局使两种手型能够共享触觉编码器。无需先学习额外跨传感器转换来关联人机接触。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; Figure 4 | policy and auxiliary learning, PDF p5

05 What human data supervises / 人类数据监督什么？
The policy builds on FTP-1, with an auxiliary language objective describing task phases. Human demonstrations support representation learning; their movements do not directly supervise the robot action output.
策略基于FTP-1，辅助语言目标描述任务阶段。人类示范辅助表征学习，不直接监督机器人动作输出。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; Figure 4 | policy and auxiliary learning, PDF p5

06 Hold robot data constant / 固定机器人数据预算
Experiments cover box reorientation, cup separation, and plug insertion across eight test conditions. Comparisons hold robot demonstrations fixed, adding one hundred fifty human demonstrations per task.
实验覆盖盒子换向、分杯、插头，共八种测试条件。对照固定机器人示教，每任务增加150条人类示范。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; fixed PDF page 6. Complete original figure, legend and caption; no graphic content redrawn.

07 Complete success improves / 完整成功率的改变
The mean across conditions rises from twenty-two point eight to forty-five point nine percent. Each policy receives forty attempts per condition; partial completion counts as failure.
各条件均值由22.8%升至45.9%。每策略每条件40次，部分完成算失败。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; fixed PDF page 6. Complete original figure, legend and caption; no graphic content redrawn.

08 Touch is not automatically enough / 有触觉，不代表总会更好
Adding tactile input alone does not improve every unfamiliar-object condition and sometimes reduces performance. The study also uses one training seed per method and task, limiting conclusions about training variability.
只加入触觉并非所有未见条件都提升，有时反而下降。每方法每任务仅一个训练种子，训练波动尚不充分。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.02338v1; fixed PDF page 6. Complete original figure, legend and caption; no graphic content redrawn.

09 A hardware-data connection / 把传感硬件与数据连接起来
The important connection runs from a shared physical sensor layout to reusable human contact data. SoTa names the skin; it is not a claim of winning every dexterous-manipulation benchmark.
关键链路是从共享传感布局走向可复用人类接触数据。SoTa是皮肤名称，并不意味着赢得所有灵巧操作基准。
