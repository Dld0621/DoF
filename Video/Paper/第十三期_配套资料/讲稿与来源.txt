DoF Paper EP.13 | Planning Through Contact
多指接触，能不能实时规划？

01 Many contacts, one motion / 多处接触，一个动作
Turning an object inside a robot hand changes several contacts at once. This paper asks whether a compact physics model can plan those changes quickly, without learning a policy.
物体在手里转动时，多处接触同时变化。这篇用紧凑物理模型快速规划，不先训练策略。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=11

02 Why contact is expensive / 接触为什么难算
A contact can push an object, but cannot pull it through an empty gap. Encoding these switching conditions directly makes trajectory optimization difficult as the number of possible contacts grows.
接触可以推物体，隔着空隙却不能拉。直接编码这些切换条件，会让多接触优化很难。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=5

03 A closed-form correction / 闭式接触修正
Look at the green displacement: that is where external forces would move the object. The model adds a contact correction, then smooths the calculation so an optimizer can use derivatives.
绿色位移表示物体在外力下原本怎样动。再加入接触修正并平滑计算，供优化器求导。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=5

04 Plan, move, observe / 规划、执行、重观测
Model predictive control searches for short sequences of finger displacements toward the target. It executes the next command, reads the updated state, and replans instead of trusting one long prediction.
模型预测控制搜索趋向目标的短时指尖动作。执行下一步、读取状态，再重新规划。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=5

05 What the controller knows / 控制器已知什么
The experiments use seventeen object shapes with both TriFinger and Allegro in MuJoCo. Known geometry and simulator states supply contact distances and Jacobians; no real camera tracking system is evaluated.
在仿真中，两种手分别测试十七种形状。几何与状态已知，没有评测真实相机追踪。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=11

06 Read the success rule / 先读成功规则
TriFinger success allows twenty millimeters of position error and roughly twenty-three degrees of orientation error. The conditions must hold for twenty consecutive rollout steps, rather than one lucky instant.
成功允许二十毫米位置和约二十三度角误差。还要连续满足二十步，不能只瞬间接近。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=12

07 The measured result / 测出来的结果
The reported TriFinger success rate is ninety-seven percent under that simulation protocol. Its mean final position error is six point seven millimeters, while mean orientation error is about twelve degrees.
在该仿真协议下，成功率为百分之九十七。平均终态位置误差六点七毫米，角误差约十二度。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=12

08 Where the model breaks / 模型的边界
Local contact geometry can miss the global consequences of an asymmetric shape. Some self-collision constraints are also incomplete; fast optimization does not establish reliable operation on an unseen physical object.
局部接触几何可能漏掉非对称形状的全局影响。自碰撞约束也不完整，真机泛化仍需验证。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=11

09 A useful design lesson / 值得借鉴的设计
The practical contribution is making contact-aware planning computationally manageable with a compact differentiable model. For deployment, the remaining work includes state estimation, geometry uncertainty, and validating the chosen success tolerances on hardware.
贡献是用紧凑可微模型，让接触规划算得动。落地还需状态估计、几何不确定性和真机验证。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.07855v4#page=13
