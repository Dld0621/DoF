DoF Paper EP.03 | DexPilot: From Bare Hands to Robot Contact
DexPilot：裸手动作怎样变成机器人接触

01 A thin object, a difficult grasp / 薄物体，难抓取
Pulling a banknote from a wallet looks easy, until a robot tries. The fingers must pinch a thin edge without pushing it away. DexPilot asks whether a person can guide that contact using bare hands.
从钱包抽出纸币，对机器人并不简单。指尖必须捏住薄边，不能把它推走。DexPilot让人用裸手引导这次接触。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol

02 Four views become a hand estimate / 四路视角变成人手估计
Four depth cameras observe the operator, producing a fused three-dimensional point cloud. Neural networks locate the hand and provide keypoints and joint-angle priors. A model-based tracker then fits an articulated hand to those observations.
四台深度相机融合出三维点云。网络定位人手，给出关键点和关节先验。模型跟踪器再把关节手模型拟合到观测。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol

03 Copy relationships, not joint angles / 复制关系，而非关节角
Human fingers and Allegro fingers have different proportions and different motion limits. So an optimizer matches relationships between fingertips, rather than copying every angle. Nearby fingertips receive special treatment, helping the robot preserve a useful pinch.
人手和Allegro的比例与运动范围不同。优化器匹配指尖关系，而非照搬关节角。临近指尖被特殊处理，以保留有效捏合。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol.

04 Three linked processes / 三个相连的进程
The architecture separates learned perception, model tracking, and the robot control loop. Hand targets feed arm motion generation and impedance control for execution. The paper reports roughly one second across the complete system chain.
架构分开处理感知、模型跟踪和机器人控制。人手目标进入手臂运动生成与阻抗控制。论文报告整条系统链约有一秒延迟。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol.

05 Bare-hand deployment needs training labels / 裸手部署仍需要训练标签
Bare-hand operation does not mean the perception model learned without labeled examples. A colored glove helped initialize tracking and generate labels during data collection. Those tracked poses supervise a second stage that works on bare-hand point clouds.
裸手操作不等于模型没有使用标注训练。采集时，彩色手套辅助初始化与标签生成。这些姿态再监督处理裸手点云的第二阶段。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol

06 What was actually tested? / 实验究竟测试了什么
Two trained operators attempted fifteen teleoperation tasks, with five consecutive trials per task. The plots measure task success and completion time, not fingertip localization error. The human still chooses actions and reacts when a manipulation starts failing.
两名熟练操作者测试15项任务，各连续五次。图表统计成功率与耗时，不是指尖定位误差。动作选择和失败应对仍由操作者完成。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol.

07 A narrow clearance is not guaranteed accuracy / 小间隙不等于精度保证
The paper mentions insertion with a clearance of one tenth of a millimeter. But success was typically around ten percent and depended on initial placement. That example cannot establish reliable submillimeter positioning for the complete robot system.
论文提到过间隙0.1毫米的插入。但通常成功率约10%，且依赖初始摆放。这一案例不能证明系统具有稳定亚毫米精度。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol.

08 The useful lesson / 值得带走的结论
DexPilot shows why good hand tracking still needs contact-aware mapping and human feedback. It offers a practical route to demonstrations, while autonomous recovery remains separate. For precision work, judge the whole loop: observation, mapping, contact, and recovery.
DexPilot说明，追踪还需要接触映射与人反馈。它提供示教采集路线，自主恢复是另一问题。精细操作要看观测、映射、接触和恢复全链路。
Source: DexPilot, arXiv:1910.03135v2; original figures and experimental protocol
