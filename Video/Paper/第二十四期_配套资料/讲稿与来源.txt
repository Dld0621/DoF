DoF Paper EP.24 | DeCAL | Touch, Imagine, Act
DeCAL：接触之后，VLA该如何思考？

01 A hidden contact / 看不到的接触
A robot can see a bottle cap without knowing whether its fingers grip securely. DeCAL asks how a vision-language-action model should use touch before choosing its next motion.
看见瓶盖，不等于知道手指抓稳了。DeCAL研究VLA如何用触觉决定下一步。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Figure 1 | overview, PDF p1

02 Six physical tasks / 六项真机任务
The paper tests wiping, assembly, twisting, pipetting, and screwing a light bulb. These tasks require coordinated arm movement and small finger adjustments under changing contact.
论文测试擦拭、装配、拧盖、移液和拧灯泡。任务需要手臂与手指随接触变化协同调整。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Table 1 | real tasks, PDF p7

03 Gate the touch / 按接触调节信息
First, fingertip deformation maps become tactile tokens describing local and global contact. A contact-aware gate changes their contribution, rather than mixing every sensory input equally.
首先，把指尖形变编码为局部和全局触觉特征。接触门控调节触觉权重，不把各种信号等量混合。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Figure 2 | architecture, PDF p4

04 Imagine the consequence / 预测动作后的接触
Second, a generation expert predicts future visual and tactile representations together. Learning what contact should become supplies the action model with information about physical change.
第二，生成专家联合预测未来视觉与触觉表征。学习接触如何变化，为动作模型提供物理线索。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Figure 2 | architecture, PDF p4

05 Coordinate arm and fingers / 分别建模，协调执行
Third, factorized flow matching models arm and hand actions with separate noise streams. The streams interact, preserving coordination while allowing fine finger motion to have distinct dynamics.
第三，分解流匹配分别处理手臂和手指动作。两路共同交互，兼顾协同与手指精细动态。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Figure 2 | architecture, PDF p4

06 What was actually tested / 具体实验协议
The platform uses two UR5 arms and two twenty-two-degree-of-freedom SharpaWave hands. Each task has one hundred demonstrations and twenty evaluation trials for each method.
平台使用双UR5和两只22自由度SharpaWave手。每项任务100条示教，每种方法评测20次。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Experimental protocol | PDF p6

07 Read the complete success / 读懂完整成功率
Across six tasks, complete success averages seventy point eight percent. DECO reaches fifty-five point eight percent under the same protocol: a fifteen-point difference.
六项任务的完整成功率平均为70.8%。同协议DECO为55.8%，相差15个百分点。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Table 1 | real tasks, PDF p7

08 Progress is not completion / 阶段进展与任务完成
The reported progress score also credits completed stages of unfinished attempts. Keep that separate from complete success; the light-bulb task still succeeds only forty percent.
进度得分也会计入未完成尝试中的成功阶段。要与完整成功分开；拧灯泡仍只有40%成功。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Table 1 | real tasks, PDF p7

09 The useful lesson / 值得借鉴的设计
DeCAL combines contact-dependent sensing, future physical representations, and coordinated action generation. Its evidence comes from this instrumented platform and small task suite, not arbitrary household robots.
DeCAL结合接触门控、未来表征与协同动作生成。证据来自这套传感平台与任务集，泛化仍需检验。
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.09119v1; Figure 1 | overview, PDF p1
