Baseline、GT 与公平评测
Baselines, Ground Truth, and Fair Evaluation

Which result would you trust? / 什么结果才可信？
Two robot hands imitate the same pinch, but only one carries the cup successfully. Which method is better depends on what we agreed to measure. A pose mapper, a contact planner, and a controller solve different problems. We need a protocol that identifies the contribution of each component. Start by freezing the question, the inputs, and the definition of success. Then compare evidence at the correct layer, while retaining every failed attempt.
两只机器人手模仿同一个捏合动作，却只有一只成功搬走杯子。哪种方法更好，取决于我们事先约定测什么。姿态映射器、接触规划器和控制器，解决的是不同问题。我们需要用评测协议，分清每个组件的贡献。首先固定问题、输入，以及成功的定义。随后在对应层级比较证据，并保留每一次失败。

Two experiments, two claims / 两种实验，两种结论
A native experiment asks whether a paper works under its original setup. Reproduce its sensors, hand model, controller, data selection, and evaluation rules. A controlled comparison asks which component works better with shared conditions. For example, feed identical tracked keypoints into several hand mapping objectives. That is an added benchmark, not automatically a reproduction of every original system. List every adaptation, including extra smoothing and newly supplied object information.
原生实验回答：论文在原本的配置下是否有效。需要保留其传感器、手模型、控制器、数据筛选和评价规则。受控比较则问：在共同条件下，哪个组件更有效。例如，把同一份追踪关键点送进不同的手部映射目标。这是新增基准，并不自动等于复现了每套原系统。列出额外平滑、新增物体信息等每项改动。
https://arxiv.org/html/2202.10448v2
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.16272v2

Build a baseline ladder / 建立分层 baseline
Begin with calibrated joint mapping, fingertip position, vectors, and pinch-aware geometry. Next compare learned mappings, such as GeoRT, against a clearly specified optimizer. Object-aware methods add information that a hand-only baseline never receives. Finally, physical transfer and feedback policies must be evaluated through executed motion. Keep these groups visible instead of merging them into one performance ranking. A fair comparison states whether it tests the objective, implementation, or complete system.
从标定后的关节映射、指尖位置、向量和捏合几何开始。然后将 GeoRT 等学习映射，与定义清楚的优化器比较。物体感知方法加入了纯手部 baseline 从未得到的信息。最后，物理迁移和反馈策略，需要通过实际执行的运动评价。应保留这些分组，而不是合成一个性能排行榜。公平比较要说明：测试的是目标函数、实现，还是完整系统。

https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07541v1

What does ground truth mean? / GT 到底指什么？
Accurate human motion does not provide a unique correct robot joint configuration. Different finger lengths, joints, and contact surfaces change which solutions are possible. A robot reference generated by optimization inherits that optimizer and its assumptions. A paired gesture chosen by a person is a correspondence, not universal truth. Record whether each label was measured, reconstructed, manually selected, or simulated. This prevents a model from appearing correct simply because it imitates its label generator.
准确的人体动作，并不能给出唯一正确的机器人关节配置。不同的指长、关节和接触表面，会改变可行解。优化生成的机器人参考，也继承了优化器及其假设。由人选定的配对手势，是一种对应关系，不是普适真值。应记录每类标签来自测量、重建、人工选择，还是仿真。这样才不会把模仿标签生成器本身，误当成方法正确的证明。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.11200
https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07541v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08341v1

Contact labels are not force labels / 接触标签不是力标签
ContactPose combines grasp poses, object poses, images, and thermal contact observations. It helps ask where a hand touches an object during a grasp. The thermal map is not a measurement of time-varying grip forces. Its documentation also marks unobserved surface regions as unknown, not confirmed non-contact. Respect that mask when evaluating contact precision and recall. A selected grasp subset must be identified separately from the complete dataset.
ContactPose 结合抓握姿态、物体姿态、图像和热成像接触观测。它帮助我们研究：抓握时，手接触了物体的哪里。这张热接触图，不是随时间变化的抓握力测量。其文档还把未观测表面标成未知，而不是确认没有接触。计算接触精确率与召回率时，要保留这一有效性掩码。经过筛选的抓握子集，也必须与完整数据集区分。
https://contactpose.cc.gatech.edu/
https://github.com/facebookresearch/ContactPose/blob/main/docs/doc.md

Motion datasets provide human references / 动作数据提供人体参考
GRAB fits body, hand, and object motion from captured markers. Its contact information is computed from reconstructed geometry, rather than measured robot forces. OakInk2 adds bimanual manipulation and structured descriptions of complex tasks. ARCTIC includes two interacting hands and articulated objects with changing internal configuration. These datasets can provide source trajectories, reconstruction targets, or task references. None automatically supplies successful robot controls for your particular hand and controller.
GRAB 根据采集到的标记点，拟合身体、手和物体运动。其接触信息由重建几何计算得到，而非测量机器人接触力。OakInk2 提供双手操作，以及复杂任务的结构化描述。ARCTIC 包含交互的双手，以及内部构型会变化的关节物体。这些数据可以提供源轨迹、重建目标，或任务参考。它们都不会自动提供适合你那只手和控制器的成功机器人控制。
https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.11200
https://oakink.net/v2/
https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Fan_ARCTIC_A_Dataset_for_Dexterous_Bimanual_Hand-Object_Manipulation_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf

Vision labels need their own audit / 视觉标签也需要审计
DexYCB provides images and hand-object pose annotations for perception benchmarks. Using those human poses for retargeting does not create robot action labels. HO-Cap uses multiple RGB-D cameras and a headset during data collection. Its hand and object shapes and poses are produced through semi-automatic annotation. Separate training, calibration, reference generation, and held-out evaluation for each experiment. Split by people, objects, or sequences deliberately; neighboring video frames can leak information.
DexYCB 提供图像与手物姿态标注，用于感知基准。将这些人体姿态用于重定向，并不会自动得到机器人动作标签。HO-Cap 在采集时使用多台 RGB-D 相机和头戴设备。其中手和物体的形状、姿态，通过半自动标注获得。每个实验都应区分训练、标定、参考生成和留出评测的角色。应明确按人、物体或序列划分；相邻视频帧可能造成信息泄漏。
https://dex-ycb.github.io/assets/chao_cvpr2021.pdf
https://openreview.net/pdf?id=hpu6r8oLw9

Freeze the input contract / 固定输入协议
Save the exact keypoints, timestamps, confidence values, and missing-data masks once. Every mapper receives the same stream, coordinate conventions, and scale calibration. Freeze the robot geometry, independent joints, coupling rules, and joint limits. State whether the wrist is fixed, freely moving, or controlled by an arm. A free wrist can reduce fingertip error by moving the entire hand. That freedom must not appear in only one method without being explicitly reported.
首先保存完全相同的关键点、时间戳、置信度和缺失掩码。让所有映射器接收同一数据流、坐标约定和尺度标定。固定机器人几何、独立关节、联动关系和关节限位。说明手腕是固定、自由运动，还是由机械臂控制。自由手腕可以通过移动整只手，降低指尖误差。这种自由度不能只给一个方法，却不明确报告。

Account for every compute budget / 计算预算要算完整
Give online solvers the same time budget and document initialization and stopping rules. Report training data, reusable pretraining, and adaptation costs separately for learned methods. A small inference cost does not erase a large preparation cost. Likewise, learning without paired robot labels does not mean learning without data. Include human gesture anchors and simulator-generated samples when those are required. Freeze tuning trials and evaluate final choices on data untouched during selection.
在线求解器应使用相同时间预算，并记录初始化与停止规则。学习方法应分别报告训练数据、可复用预训练和任务适配。推理成本很低，并不意味着准备成本消失了。同样，不需要配对机器人标签，不等于不需要数据。需要人工手势锚点和仿真样本时，也应计入。固定调参次数，并在选择过程中从未使用的数据上测试最终配置。

Read geometry and task metrics together / 几何与任务指标一起看
The original AKO Figure Five separates geometry errors above from task results below. Global fingertip error changes with the wrist; wrist-relative error removes that translation. Thumb-relative error focuses on the finger relationships involved in pinching. The figure shows why the smallest geometric error need not imply the best task result. Always label the frame, units, target construction, and aggregation rule. Evaluate independent task outcomes too, rather than scoring only the loss a method optimizes.
AKO 原论文 Figure 5，把上方几何误差和下方任务结果分开显示。全局指尖误差会受手腕影响；腕相对误差消除了共同平移。拇指相对误差，更关注捏合涉及的手指关系。这张图说明：几何误差最小，并不一定意味着任务结果最好。必须标明坐标系、单位、目标构造和聚合规则。还要评价独立任务结果，而不是只测方法自己优化的损失。

Contact needs more than one score / 接触需要不止一个分数
TopoRetarget Figure Three separates misplaced or missing contact, penetration, and overextension. These are visibly different failures, so a single fingertip score cannot describe them. For contact F-one, specify the correspondence, distance threshold, and valid surface region. It combines contact precision and recall, but does not measure force stability. Report penetration depth and how often it exceeds the chosen tolerance. A small average can hide a brief, severe collision during a crucial contact transition.
TopoRetarget Figure 3，区分接触错位、接触缺失、穿透和过度伸展。这些失败在视觉上就不同，单一指尖分数无法完整描述。计算接触 F1，要定义对应关系、距离阈值和有效表面区域。它综合接触精确率和召回率，却不测量受力稳定性。还应报告穿透深度，以及超过预定容差的频率。很小的平均值，也可能掩盖关键接触切换中的短暂严重碰撞。

Smoothness and latency are a pair / 平滑与延迟要一起测
Estimate joint velocity, acceleration, and jerk using the same timestamps and filtering choices. Jerk measures how quickly acceleration changes; noisy differentiation can inflate it. Stronger smoothing may reduce jitter while delaying the motion an operator requested. Measure delay from camera exposure to the robot response, not just neural inference. Report median and tail latency, dropouts, and timeouts on the stated hardware. Generation throughput, control frequency, and end-to-end delay describe different quantities.
用同样的时间戳和滤波设置，估计关节速度、加速度和 jerk。Jerk 衡量加速度变化的快慢；带噪微分可能把它放大。更强平滑可能降低抖动，却推迟操作者要求的动作。延迟应从相机曝光测到机器人响应，而不只测网络推理。应在指定硬件上报告中位延迟、尾部延迟、掉帧和超时。生成吞吐、控制频率和端到端延迟，是不同的量。

Define the whole physical event / 定义完整物理事件
For a cup transfer, record contact, load-bearing lift, transport, placement, and release separately. Define the required clearance, hold duration, target region, and timeout before testing. A brief lift cannot stand in for completing the entire sequence. Freeze object geometry, mass, friction, controller gains, and simulation settings across methods. Artificial support forces must be removed before judging unaided physical execution. Separate simulated and real results, including resets, dropped objects, and recovery attempts.
搬运杯子时，要分别记录接触、承重提起、运输、放置和释放。测试前定义离地高度、保持时长、目标区域和超时条件。短暂提起，不能代替完成整个动作序列。不同方法之间，应固定物体几何、质量、摩擦、控制增益和仿真设置。评价无辅助物理执行之前，必须移除人为支撑力。仿真和真机实验应分开报告，并包含复位、掉落和恢复尝试。
https://arxiv.org/html/2503.21860v1
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.09484v3

Keep the failures in the denominator / 失败必须留在分母里
ManipTrans reports tracking errors on successful trajectories, alongside its separate success rates. That qualification matters whenever two methods succeed on different subsets. A method can show low conditional error after failing on every difficult example. Keep successful-only error, overall completion rate, and the number of attempts visible together. Preserve failure videos and termination reasons, rather than silently discarding unusable rollouts. If comparing errors on a shared successful subset, label that additional selection explicitly.
ManipTrans 在成功轨迹上报告跟踪误差，同时另外报告成功率。两种方法成功的样本子集不同时，这个限定尤其重要。如果困难样本全部失败，方法仍可能显示很低的条件误差。要同时展示仅成功样本误差、整体完成率，以及尝试次数。应保留失败视频和终止原因，不能默默丢掉无法使用的执行。如果还比较共同成功子集上的误差，也要明确标注这层筛选。
https://arxiv.org/html/2503.21860v1

People and perturbations change results / 人和扰动也会改变结果
In teleoperation, people compensate for mapping errors, so operator experience affects the outcome. Report practice time, repeated trials, task order, and whether order was counterbalanced. Keep camera views, visual feedback, and latency comparable across conditions. Test new people with shared occlusion, tracking noise, and initial object displacement. Report variation across people and seeds, with uncertainty estimated at the trial level. Thousands of neighboring video frames are not thousands of independent manipulation trials.
遥操作中，人会补偿映射误差，因此操作者经验会影响结果。应报告练习时间、重复次数、任务顺序，以及是否做了顺序平衡。各条件下的相机视角、视觉反馈和延迟，应尽可能一致。测试留出的操作者，以及共同的遮挡、追踪噪声和物体初态偏移。报告不同操作者和随机种子的波动，并在试验层面估计不确定性。数千个相邻视频帧，并不等于数千次独立操作试验。
https://arxiv.org/html/2202.10448v2
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.08341v1

Choose for your camera-only system / 为无穿戴系统选型
For a camera-only system, first stabilize visible hand tracking and coordinate alignment. Establish a geometric baseline, then locate the dominant failure before adding complexity. Use learned mapping when its response and preparation costs fit your deployment. Add object constraints for interaction errors, and feedback control for physical execution errors. Treat published best results as protocol-specific evidence, never a universal winner. The useful answer is which component improves your complete task under a reproducible test.
无穿戴摄像头系统，首先要稳定可见手部追踪和坐标对齐。先建立几何 baseline，再定位主要失败，决定是否增加复杂度。学习映射的响应与准备成本适合部署时，再选择它。交互几何出错时加入物体约束，物理执行出错时加入反馈控制。论文里的最佳结果，是特定协议下的证据，不是普适冠军。真正有用的答案，是哪个组件在可复现测试中改善了你的完整任务。
