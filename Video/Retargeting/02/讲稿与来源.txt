灵巧手重定向基线：从关节映射到捏合与方向约束
Retargeting Baselines: From Joint Mapping to Pinch-Aware Objectives

Same angles, different grasp / 角度相同，抓法不同
Copy the human finger angles, and the robot still misses the cup. The problem appears even when the human pose is perfectly measured. Robot fingers have different lengths, joint axes, and thumb geometry. Direct mapping assigns each human angle a robot joint, scale, and offset. Calibration aligns the direction, neutral pose, and permitted motion range. It is a useful baseline because its assumptions are visible. Matching angles, however, does not preserve where the fingertips meet.
复制人手弯曲角，机器人却仍然碰不到杯子。即使人手姿态测量完全准确，这个问题也会出现。机器人的指长、关节轴和拇指结构都不同。直接映射给人手角度指定对应机器人关节、比例和偏移。标定负责统一方向、零位和允许的运动范围。它适合作为基础对照，因为每个假设都很清楚。但是角度相同，并不意味着指尖在同一位置汇合。

Optimize the joints you actually control / 只优化真正可控的关节
Before choosing a loss, define which robot variables can actually move independently. Some distal joints follow another joint through a mechanical coupling. Their angle is a scaled source angle plus a fixed offset. The optimizer should respect that relationship while computing fingertip positions. Optimizing both freely and overriding one afterward invalidates the predicted geometry. Joint limits constrain the search, while joint names determine command ordering. A convincing animation can still be wrong if either mapping is incorrect.
选择损失之前，先确定哪些机器人关节能够独立运动。有些末端关节会通过机械联动跟随另一个关节。它的角度等于源关节角乘比例，再加固定偏移。优化器计算指尖位置时，就应该遵守这种关系。先自由优化两个关节、执行时再覆盖其中一个，会破坏预测几何。关节限位约束搜索范围，关节名称决定控制命令的顺序。这两种映射只要出错，再像样的动画也可能是错的。

Position IK asks where, not how / 位置IK：到哪儿，怎么弯
Position inverse kinematics changes the question: where should each robot fingertip go? For a candidate joint configuration, forward kinematics predicts those positions. The objective adds the weighted errors between predicted and target fingertips. A posture penalty can favor a comfortable configuration among similar solutions. A temporal penalty discourages large changes from the previous frame. But the same endpoint may permit different intermediate finger shapes. One shape clears the cup; another can bend through it.
位置逆运动学换了一个问题：机器人各指尖应该去哪里？给定候选关节配置，正向运动学就能算出这些位置。目标函数累加预测指尖与目标指尖之间的加权误差。姿态惩罚可以在相近解中优先选择更自然的配置。时间惩罚则抑制相邻帧之间过大的关节变化。然而，同一个指尖位置可以对应不同的中间指节姿态。一种姿态绕开杯子，另一种却可能弯进杯子里。

Vectors preserve more than distances / 向量比距离多保留什么
A distance tells us how far apart two landmarks are. It cannot tell us which direction connects those two landmarks. A unit direction provides direction, but deliberately removes the length. A full vector preserves both, after the human and robot axes are aligned. Subtracting two positions cancels translation; it does not cancel rotation. This is why relative vectors still need consistent coordinate conventions. Choose the representation according to the relationship you need to preserve.
距离告诉我们两个关键点相隔多远。但它不能告诉我们连接两个关键点的方向。单位方向保留朝向，却主动去掉长度信息。完整向量在统一人机坐标轴后，同时保留长度和方向。两个位置相减可以抵消平移，但不能抵消旋转。所以，即使使用相对向量，坐标系约定仍必须一致。应该根据要保留的几何关系，选择具体表示。

The edge set is the method / 选哪些连线，决定保留什么
Vector retargeting matches selected human landmark vectors to robot forward kinematics. Wrist to fingertip vectors describe the overall shape of the hand. Thumb to finger vectors describe coordination between opposing fingertips. The edge set, scaling, and weights are part of the baseline definition. AnyTeleop separates this hand mapping from its arm motion generation. Comparing entire teleoperation systems also changes perception, planning, and feedback. We cannot attribute every improvement in that comparison to a hand loss.
向量重定向把选定的人手向量与机器人正向运动学结果对齐。腕到指尖的向量描述手的整体形状。拇指到其他指尖的向量描述对指协调关系。连线集合、尺度和权重，都是基线定义的一部分。AnyTeleop把这种手部映射与机械臂运动生成分开。比较整套遥操作系统时，感知、规划和反馈也会变化。不能把这种比较中的全部提升都归功于手部损失。

DexMV adds the middle of the finger / DexMV：别只盯着指尖
DexMV makes the ambiguity concrete in this original comparison figure. The middle row uses fingertip mapping; inspect the fingers around each object. The bottom row adds information about intermediate finger geometry. Its task-space vectors represent fingertips and middle phalanges relative to the palm. That gives the optimizer information about bending, beyond the final fingertip location. The lesson is to constrain an ambiguous shape with additional landmarks. This geometric improvement still needs separate validation during physical object manipulation.
DexMV在这张原始对比图中展示了这种歧义。中间一行使用指尖映射，请看每个物体周围的手指。最下面一行加入了手指中间部分的几何信息。它的任务空间向量描述指尖和中间指节相对手掌的关系。这样，优化器得到的不只是指尖终点，还有弯曲信息。这里的关键是用额外关键点，约束原本存在歧义的手形。这种几何改善，仍需要在物体操作中另外验证。

A trajectory is not yet an action / 关节轨迹还不是动作
Now follow the lower half of DexMV's original pipeline figure. Human pose first becomes a sequence of robot joint configurations. A separate action estimation stage converts that sequence into robot commands. The retargeting objective also penalizes changes from the previous joint configuration. Starting the next solve from the previous solution is another useful choice. These choices promote continuity, but do not prove that a grasp can support weight. We must still distinguish a matched posture from an executable manipulation.
现在沿着DexMV原始流程图的下半部分看。人手姿态首先被转换为一串机器人关节配置。随后，独立的动作估计阶段把轨迹变成机器人命令。重定向目标还会惩罚相对于上一帧的关节变化。把上一帧结果作为下一次求解的起点，也是有用的选择。这些做法帮助保持连续，却不能证明抓取能够承重。仍然需要区分姿态匹配和可执行的操作。

DexPilot prioritizes a pinch / DexPilot：优先保住捏合
DexPilot focuses on a different failure: fingertips that almost meet but cannot pinch. Look at its original vector diagram for the human and Allegro hands. One group connects the palm to the fingertips. Another connects the primary fingertips toward the opposing thumb. A third group relates primary fingers when they close toward the thumb together. These groups do not always receive the same target lengths or priorities. The objective changes near a pinch to favor coordinated closure.
DexPilot关注另一种失败：指尖快碰到了，却仍然无法捏合。请看它在人手和Allegro手上绘制的原始向量图。一组连线连接手掌和各个指尖。另一组从主指指尖指向对侧拇指。第三组描述多根主指同时向拇指闭合时的相互关系。这些向量组并不总是使用相同的目标长度和优先级。接近捏合时，目标会调整，以优先促进协调闭合。
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.03135v2

Projection is an intended target / 投影是目标，不是接触测量
For an open hand, the original DexPilot scales ordinary vector lengths. Near pinching, selected thumb-related targets become very short and receive greater weight. The specified primary target length is one tenth of a millimeter. That is an optimization target, not measured sensor accuracy or contact force. The original paper does not publish a numerical switching threshold. It also reports that projection can complicate small-object release. Helping fingers close can therefore create a different problem when opening.
开手状态下，原版DexPilot对普通向量长度进行缩放。接近捏合时，选定的拇指相关目标变得很短，并获得更高权重。原文给出的主投影目标长度是零点一毫米。这是优化目标，不是传感器精度，也不是接触力。原始论文没有公布具体的切换阈值数值。论文也指出，投影可能使小物体的释放更难控制。因此，帮助闭合的机制，也可能给张手带来另一个问题。
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.03135v2

The library version is a different baseline / 库里的版本，需要单独标注
The current dex-retargeting library adapts DexPilot, rather than copying it unchanged. Its default projection enters below three centimeters and exits above five. The intermediate region preserves the previous projection state, creating hysteresis. It also changes loss behavior and regularizes toward the previous configuration. The original paper instead regularizes Allegro joint angles toward zero. A benchmark labeled only DexPilot can therefore hide materially different implementations. Record the repository revision, selected mode, and complete configuration before comparing results.
当前dex-retargeting库改编了DexPilot，并非原封不动地复制。默认设置在小于三厘米时进入投影，大于五厘米时退出。中间区间保留上一时刻的投影状态，形成滞回。它还改变损失形式，并向上一帧关节配置正则化。原论文则把Allegro关节角向零位正则化。所以只写DexPilot这个名字，可能掩盖实质不同的实现。比较结果之前，要记录代码版本、选定模式和完整配置。

AKO separates the objectives / AKO：把目标逐项拆开
Analyzing Key Objectives asks which geometric requirements actually matter for manipulation. Its original diagram separates global pose, overall shape, fingertip relationships, and direction. The full objective combines those requirements with posture and temporal regularization. This is also an arm-and-hand problem, allowing both to contribute. A global thumb target anchors a contact-relevant point in space. Wrist orientation remains a softer preference, rather than fixing everything at the wrist. The terms express priorities that can be tested through controlled ablations.
Analyzing Key Objectives研究的是：哪些几何要求真正影响操作？原图把全局姿态、整体手形、指尖关系和末端方向分开展示。完整目标把这些要求与姿态正则、时间正则结合起来。它同时优化机械臂和手，让两者共同调整。全局拇指目标把一个与接触密切相关的点锚定在空间中。腕部朝向则作为较弱偏好，而不是把一切都固定在手腕上。这些项表达的优先级，可以通过受控消融实验来检验。

Smoothly trade shape for pinch / 让手形与捏合平稳过渡
The pinch term compares the robot's thumb-to-finger vector with a modified human target. It preserves the human direction while changing the requested distance. Below the lower threshold, the target distance is zero. Between two thresholds, a continuous linear rule compresses the distance. Above the upper threshold, it keeps the measured distance. Complementary weights adjust the relative priority of hand shape and pinching. This is a continuous geometric preference, not a measurement of physical contact.
捏合项比较机器人拇指到主指的向量，与修改后的人手目标。它保留人手方向，同时改变要求的距离。低于下阈值时，目标距离为零。两个阈值之间，用连续的线性规则压缩距离。高于上阈值时，则保留测得的距离。互补权重调整整体手形与捏合之间的相对优先级。这是一种连续的几何偏好，并不是实际接触测量。

Where the last finger segment points / 最后一节手指朝哪里
A fingertip can reach the correct position while facing the wrong way. AKO therefore compares vectors from the last finger joint to the fingertip. Watch the original mug-handle example: the thumb must bend to form a hook. Position alone does not describe the useful shape of that hook. However, one segment direction does not determine a complete three-dimensional orientation. Actual contact normals also depend on the fingertip surface and contact location. Treat this term as an orientation cue, not a replacement for contact modeling.
指尖可以到达正确位置，却朝向错误方向。因此AKO还比较从最后一个指关节到指尖的向量。请看原图中的杯把示例：拇指必须弯曲才能形成钩子。单靠位置，无法描述这个钩子所需的形状。但一根指段的方向，不能决定完整的三维姿态。实际接触法向还取决于指尖表面和接触位置。应该把这一项理解为方向线索，而不是接触建模的替代品。

Ablations answer different questions / 消融实验各自回答什么
Read the ablations as specific questions about the full objective. A1 removes pinching; A2 keeps raw pinch distances without compression. A3 removes fingertip direction; A4 substitutes a wrist-to-distal-joint vector. A5 replaces the global thumb target with a wrist target. A6 additionally removes the pinch and fingertip-direction terms. A7 removes posture regularization; A8 removes it from the simpler A6 configuration. These changes test mechanisms; they are not eight independently published algorithms.
应该把消融实验读成针对完整目标的具体问题。A1去掉捏合项；A2保留未经压缩的真实捏合距离。A3去掉指尖方向；A4把它换成手腕到末端关节的向量。A5把全局拇指位置目标换成手腕目标。A6在此基础上，再去掉捏合项和指尖方向项。A7去掉姿态正则；A8则在更简化的A6基础上去掉它。这些改动用于检验机制，并不是八种独立发表的算法。

The solver is not the objective / 求解器不是目标函数
Several methods use SLSQP, but that does not make their behavior equivalent. The objective defines a good configuration; the solver searches for one. Warm starting initializes that search from the previous result. A temporal penalty changes the objective itself, discouraging rapid motion. An output filter modifies the command after optimization and can add delay. Joint limits and mechanical couplings must remain consistent across the comparison. Otherwise, a change credited to the loss may actually come from another component.
多种方法都使用SLSQP，但这并不意味着行为相同。目标函数定义什么配置更好，求解器负责寻找这种配置。暖启动用上一帧结果作为搜索起点。时间惩罚直接改变目标函数，抑制过快变化。输出滤波则在优化之后修改命令，并可能增加延迟。比较各方法时，关节限位和机械联动约束必须保持一致。否则，看似来自损失的变化，实际可能来自其他模块。

Choose baselines by the failure / 按照失败现象选择基线
In this original result figure, geometric errors and task outcomes occupy separate rows. A smaller position error does not automatically produce a more reliable manipulation. Start with calibrated joint mapping, then compare position and vector objectives. Add intermediate landmarks for bending ambiguity, and pinch terms for closure problems. Compare direction and wrist flexibility when the task depends on contact geometry. Finally, test whether the robot lifts, transports, and releases the object successfully. That last boundary takes us from geometric retargeting to physical manipulation.
这张原始结果图，把几何误差和任务结果分成了两行。位置误差更小，并不自动意味着操作更可靠。先建立标定正确的关节映射，再比较位置和向量目标。弯曲有歧义时加入中间关键点；闭合有问题时加入捏合项。当任务依赖接触几何时，再比较末端方向和手腕调整能力。最后检验机器人能否成功提起、运送和释放物体。这最后一道界限，才把几何重定向连接到真实操作。
