世界模型 01｜机器人行动之前，怎样预测后果？
World Models 01 · Before a Robot Moves

Before the robot moves / 机器人行动之前
A robot faces a cup near the edge of a table. It could approach the handle, push the cup, or move its camera to see the hidden side. Each choice changes what happens next, even though the starting image is identical. A world model gives the robot a way to predict those consequences before committing to an action. This course follows that cup from a camera observation through imagined futures, action selection, and a real outcome. The cup sequence is our teaching example, not a claimed experiment from a paper.
机器人面前有一只杯子，靠近桌子边缘。它可以靠近杯柄、推动杯子，或者移动相机看看被遮挡的一侧。起始图像完全相同，不同选择却会带来不同后果。世界模型让机器人有机会在行动之前预测这些后果。这套课程围绕这只杯子，从相机观测讲到预测未来、选择动作和实际结果。杯子场景是我们的教学示例，不是某篇论文已经完成的实验。

An image is not the full state / 一张图像，不等于完整状态
The camera measures light arriving from visible surfaces; it does not directly measure everything that determines motion. A cup can look stationary in one frame while actually sliding toward the edge. Its mass, friction, contents, and contact with the gripper may also be partly hidden. We call the available sensor reading an observation, and the information needed to predict the process its state. A learned internal state is an estimate, not a privileged view of the complete physical world.
相机测量可见表面传来的光，并不能直接测到决定运动的一切因素。杯子在一帧中看起来静止，实际上却可能正滑向桌边。它的质量、摩擦、内部物品和与夹爪的接触，也可能部分不可见。传感器能提供的读数叫观测；预测过程所需的信息构成状态。模型内部学到的状态是一种估计，并不是对整个物理世界的直接透视。
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/hafner19a/hafner19a.pdf

History changes what the robot knows / 历史观测改变判断
Two images taken a moment apart can reveal the direction of the sliding cup. Joint sensors and the previous gripper command add information that the camera alone may miss. The model combines these measurements with its memory to update an internal belief about the current situation. If two different physical states still fit the evidence, uncertainty should remain rather than vanish by assumption. A moving camera may therefore improve the next decision even when it makes no immediate progress toward grasping.
相隔片刻的两张图像，可以揭示杯子的滑动方向。关节传感器和之前的夹爪指令，还能补充单靠相机看不到的信息。模型结合这些测量和记忆，更新对当前情况的内部判断。如果两种不同物理状态都符合已有证据，就应保留不确定性，而不是假定它已经消失。因此，移动相机即使没有立刻推进抓取，也可能改善下一次决策。

An action needs an exact meaning / 动作必须有明确含义
Predicting what happens after an action requires knowing exactly what that action means. Move right could mean an image displacement, a joint target, or a Cartesian command in the robot base frame. The time interval matters too: the same velocity applied for different durations produces different motion. A useful robot model needs a documented control interface, consistent units, and synchronized observations and commands. Without that agreement, an impressive predicted video cannot tell the controller what signal to send.
预测动作的后果，首先要知道这个动作究竟是什么意思。向右移动，可能指图像位移、关节目标，也可能指机器人基坐标系中的笛卡尔指令。时间间隔同样重要：相同速度持续不同时间，会产生不同运动。可用于机器人的模型，需要明确的控制接口、一致的单位，以及同步的观测和指令。缺少这些约定，再惊艳的预测视频，也无法告诉控制器该发送什么信号。

The future may be a distribution / 未来可能是一种分布
Suppose the robot pushes a cup whose underside it has never seen. Different friction values could produce several plausible stopping positions from the same visible starting point. This ambiguity comes from incomplete information; genuine process variation can add another source of uncertainty. Returning one average position may hide the important possibility that the cup falls off the table. Sampling possible futures can expose alternatives, but those samples are trustworthy only if the model captures the relevant variation.
假设机器人推动一只从未看过杯底的杯子。从同一个可见起点出发，不同摩擦可能对应多个合理的停止位置。这种歧义来自信息不完整；过程本身的变化还会增加另一种不确定性。只返回一个平均位置，可能掩盖杯子会掉下桌子的关键可能性。对未来采样能够展现不同可能，但只有模型捕捉到相关变化时，这些样本才有参考价值。

Read a prediction figure carefully / 先看清预测图的条件
In this original Dreamer figure, the model receives a short context and the future action sequence. The later images are predictions made without receiving the intermediate camera frames. That is an open-loop prediction test: it asks how far the model can continue from its initial information. The true and predicted rows let us inspect drift in scene layout and moving bodies. The quadruped shown here belongs to a simulated control domain; this figure is not a physical robot deployment.
在这张 Dreamer 原图中，模型接收一段初始上下文，以及后续动作序列。后续图像是在没有接收中间相机画面的情况下预测出来的。这是一种开环预测测试，检查模型从初始信息出发能向前预测多远。真实行和预测行可以帮助我们观察场景结构与运动物体的偏差。这里的四足机器人来自仿真控制环境；这张图并不是实体机器人部署实验。

Reconstruction asks a different question / 重建与预测回答不同问题
Reconstructing an image means encoding an observation and recovering information from that observation. Predicting a future means estimating something that has not yet been observed, given history and possible actions. A detailed three-dimensional cup mesh can help with geometry, but it does not by itself predict sliding or spilling. Conversely, a useful predictor may operate on compact features without ever producing a photorealistic image. Ask which future quantity the representation preserves, and which decision that quantity is intended to support.
重建图像，是把一个观测编码后，再恢复这个观测中的信息。预测未来，则是根据历史和可能动作，估计尚未观测到的内容。精细的三维杯子网格有助于理解几何，却不会自动预测滑动或泼洒。反过来，有用的预测器也可以只处理紧凑特征，从不生成照片般真实的图像。要问清楚：这种表示保留了哪种未来信息，又打算支持什么决策。

A compact state can carry the prediction / 用紧凑状态承载预测
Follow the original diagram from the observation encoder into the internal variables. The model combines a recurrent memory with a stochastic representation, then advances them using an action. A decoder can turn the internal state back into an image for training or inspection. The imagined action and value computations on the right do not require rendering every future image. This separation makes it possible to compare or learn from many compact trajectories, although every trajectory still depends on model accuracy.
沿着这张原图，从观测编码器走到内部变量。模型把递归记忆与随机表示结合起来，再通过动作推进它们。解码器可以把内部状态还原成图像，用于训练或检查。右侧想象过程中的动作与价值计算，并不需要把每个未来都渲染成图像。这种分离允许系统比较或学习大量紧凑轨迹，但每条轨迹仍然依赖模型的准确性。

Predictions are learned from transitions / 预测来自转移数据
For our cup, one useful training record contains an observation, an executed command, and the following observation. Reward and termination information can be recorded as well when the learning algorithm needs them. The model learns regularities across many such transitions, including actions that did not succeed. A dataset containing only gentle pushes provides weak evidence about collisions, drops, or unfamiliar cup materials. Imagining more rollouts does not create new physical evidence about those missing parts of the task.
对于这只杯子，一条有用的训练记录包含观测、实际执行的指令，以及下一次观测。如果学习算法需要，还可以记录奖励和回合结束信息。模型从大量这样的转移中学习规律，其中也应包括没有成功的动作。只包含轻推的数据，对碰撞、跌落或陌生杯子材质提供的证据很有限。生成更多想象轨迹，并不会凭空增加这些缺失环节的物理证据。

One route searches before acting / 一条路线：行动前搜索
One way to use the model is to propose several future command sequences and predict their consequences. For example, compare approaching the handle directly with moving around an obstacle first. A task objective scores the predicted outcomes, perhaps combining progress with penalties for approaching the table edge. The controller executes the first selected action, observes what really happened, and plans again. This repeated correction is model predictive control; searching farther is useful only when prediction quality and computation allow it.
使用模型的一种方式，是提出多组未来指令序列，预测各自的后果。例如，比较直接靠近杯柄，和先绕过障碍物再靠近。任务目标为预测结果评分，例如同时考虑进展与靠近桌边的代价。控制器执行所选序列的第一个动作，观察实际发生了什么，再重新规划。这种反复修正就是模型预测控制；只有预测质量和计算预算允许时，看得更远才有价值。
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/hafner19a/hafner19a.pdf

Another route trains a policy in imagination / 另一条路线：在想象中训练策略
Dreamer uses the learned model to train an actor and a value estimator on imagined trajectories. After observing the real environment, the actor can then choose an action from the inferred internal state. It does not need to run the same candidate-sequence search at every control step. The diagram separates real experience, the replay buffer, world-model learning, and policy improvement. Planning during action selection and using imagination during training are different computational choices, even when both rely on learned dynamics.
Dreamer 使用学到的模型，在想象轨迹上训练动作策略和价值估计器。获得真实环境观测后，策略便能根据推断出的内部状态选择动作。它不需要在每个控制步骤都执行同样的候选序列搜索。这张图区分了真实经验、回放数据、世界模型学习，以及策略改进。行动选择时规划，与训练时使用想象，是两种不同的计算安排，即使它们都依赖学到的动力学。

Where the physical evidence starts / 实体机器人证据从哪里开始
DayDreamer tested this learning approach on four physical robot platforms, including two arms, a quadruped, and a wheeled robot. The arms learned pick-and-place tasks using camera inputs and other configured signals. These experiments demonstrate that imagined training can support real control under the reported setup. They do not establish that every new robot can safely learn any task without preparation. Read the control interface, reward design, collection time, and intervention rules alongside the videos.
DayDreamer 在四种实体机器人平台上测试了这条学习路线，包括两种机械臂、四足机器人和轮式机器人。机械臂使用相机输入及其配置中的其他信号，学习抓取放置任务。这些实验证明：在论文报告的配置下，想象训练能够支持真实控制。它们并没有证明，任意新机器人都能不经准备就安全学会任意任务。观看视频时，还要结合控制接口、奖励设计、采集时间和人工干预规则一起理解。

Close the loop without trusting it blindly / 闭环修正，也要知道模型边界
Our robot predicts a stable lift, but the real cup starts slipping when the gripper accelerates. The next observation can expose the mismatch and trigger a new estimate and a revised action. That helps only if the sensors detect the problem early enough and the controller can still respond. Model confidence is not a physical guarantee, and an attractive future image is not a contact-force measurement. Keep independent limits on motion, compare predictions with measurements, and retain the failed trials for diagnosis.
机器人预测杯子能稳定抬起，真实杯子却在夹爪加速时开始滑落。下一次观测能够暴露这种不一致，触发状态更新和动作调整。但这只有在传感器足够早发现问题、控制器仍来得及响应时才有帮助。模型置信度不是物理保证，漂亮的未来图像也不是接触力测量。应独立设置运动限制，对照预测与实测，并保留失败试次以便诊断。

The map for the next five chapters / 接下来，逐层打开世界模型
We now have a precise question: given what the robot has observed and what it might do, what happens next? Next we unpack representations, recurrent state, reward prediction, and uncertainty. Then we compare online planning with policies learned in imagination, and examine video and feature-prediction systems. The final chapters connect these models to robot policies and build an evaluation protocol that keeps visual quality separate from task success. Use the companion experiments to change one assumption and see which part of the predicted outcome changes.
现在问题已经明确：给定机器人看到的内容，以及它可能采取的动作，接下来会发生什么？下一章拆开表示、递归状态、奖励预测和不确定性。随后比较在线规划和想象中学到的策略，再研究视频与特征预测系统。最后两章将这些模型接入机器人策略，并建立区分画面质量与任务成功的评测协议。你可以在伴读实验里改变一个假设，观察预测结果的哪一部分随之变化。
